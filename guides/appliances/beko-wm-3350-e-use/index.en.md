Turkish machine with a mechanical selector whose programs are marked with letters rather than numbers (A, B, X, M, C, D, F, G, J, H, P). There is no separate Start button: the program is started with the On/Off button, and the selector knob turns clockwise by itself during the wash.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday cotton clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 3.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash): powder.
- the flower mark (fabric softener (with siphon; fill to the mark, no more than 100 ml)): fabric softener (optional).

The drawer is removed by pressing the mark on the siphon (the coloured insert in the softener compartment). From left to right: fabric softener with the flower symbol and siphon, compartment II for the main wash, compartment I for the prewash. Add powder no higher than the MAX mark. The program table is printed above the drawer. The siphon must be cleaned every 30-40 washes.

## Turn the programme knob to B

Turn the programme knob **A-P** to **B**: "Cotton and linen, moderately soiled, without prewash". Program B: main wash, 3 rinses, spin. For coloured cotton 40 °C is the maximum in the table.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Switch on with the On/Off button

Leave the **e**, **Rinse Hold**, **1000/500**, **Door Open** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **On/Off**.

Switches the machine on and starts the chosen program (the manual calls this button "Start/Stop"). The door locks and the LED lights up.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 19 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. After the program the door does not open at once: the Door Open lamp lights after 1-2 minutes. The door is opened with the button with the lamp, not with a handle.
