Italian machine with a mechanical 13-position selector (numbers 1-10, A, a bleach symbol and Z) and a separate temperature knob. One manual covers the Aquamatic 6, 6T, 8T, 10T family; the controls differ from model to model.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 2 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment I

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **I** (washing powder): powder.
- the flower mark (special additives (liquid): fabric softener, bleach): fabric softener (optional).

Two compartments: for washing powder and for special additives. The additives compartment is filled with liquid products only. Bleach is poured into it as well. For the prewash the powder is put straight into the drum; if the powder does not wash out well, use the special container that is put into the drum with the powder.

## Turn the programme knob to 3

Turn the programme knob to **3**: "Quick program, 30-40 °C". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Quick program 3 (30-40 °C), load up to 2 kg.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Switch on with the on/off button

Leave the **400**, **spin off**, **door** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **on/off**.

Switching on and off. First set the program selector, then press the button.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 50 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. The door does not open right after the end of the spin: wait 2 minutes.
