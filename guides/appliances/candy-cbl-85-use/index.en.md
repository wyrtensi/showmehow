Candy front loader (5 kg, 850 rpm) with a programme selector that turns both ways and has an OFF position, separate START and door buttons, four option buttons (Super Rapid, Crease Guard, Extra Rinse, Intensive) and a four-compartment drawer with bleach. The manual does not draw the knob, so its marks are rebuilt from the programme table.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash and 32-minute programme): powder or gel.
- the flower mark (additives: softener, starch, perfumes (liquid only)): softener (optional).

The drawer has 4 compartments: I powder for prewash; II powder for the main programmes and the 32-minute programme; triangle bleach; flower special additives (softeners, perfumes, starch, bluing). Only liquids go into the third and fourth compartments. For liquid detergent put the supplied container into compartment II. The "32" scoop for the rapid programme is supplied. The order left to right is taken from the drawing.

## Select programme 40

Turn the programme knob to **40**: "Non-fast coloureds 40 °C". Non-fast coloureds, cotton, up to 5 kg.

## Set the spin to max

Turn the spin knob to **max**.

## Press START

Leave the **Super Rapid (D)**, **Crease Guard (E)**, **Extra Rinse (F)**, **Intensive wash (G)**, **Door open (B)** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **START**.

Button C START: starts the selected programme; it may take a few seconds to begin. To change settings of a running programme (pause), release START, make the change and press START again. To open the door or add laundry, release START and wait 2 minutes. The indicator light comes on {{led green}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 38 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. START is a latching button. To open the door, add laundry or cancel a programme, release START first and wait about 2 minutes. To cancel: release START, turn the knob to OFF, wait 5 seconds.
