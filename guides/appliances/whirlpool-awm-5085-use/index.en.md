Whirlpool front-loader with a programme selector that turns by itself during the cycle, separate temperature and spin selectors, an On/Off button that also starts the wash and three function buttons (Eco, Quick, Anti-crease). No display; a short programme list is printed on the panel.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday cotton clothes, 40 C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (compartment II: detergent for the main wash; stain removers, water softener (hardness 4)): powder or liquid detergent.
- the flower mark (flower: softeners and finishes (not above MAX, about 100 ml), starch dissolved in water): softener if you like.

The drawer pulls out towards you; depending on the model it has three or four compartments. The left-to-right order is not shown in the drawing and is assumed. Liquid detergent for the main wash: take out the coloured insert and put it into compartment II (the graduated scale helps with dosing). For highly concentrated powders and liquids use the dosing ball placed straight into the drum.

## Turn the programme knob to 4

Turn the programme knob **P** to **4**: "Cotton: bedding, table and underlinen, towels, shirts, lightly to normally soiled". Cotton: main wash, rinses and spin.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Set the spin to 800 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **800** rpm.

## Switch on with the On/Off button

Leave the **Eco function**, **Quick function**, **Anti-crease function (rinse hold)** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **On/Off**.

Button A "On/Off": switches the machine on and starts the chosen programme. The indicator lamp lights. Pressing it again pauses the programme, once more it continues. To change the programme press it, choose the new programme and press it again. The indicator light comes on {{led green}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 23 min. The machine stops by itself and drains.
