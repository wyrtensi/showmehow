A narrow (32 cm deep) machine with a mechanical selector of programmes marked with the letters A-P and a separate temperature knob. Programmes H-N always end with water in the tub, and it has to be drained manually.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 3.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash powder): powder.
- the flower mark (rinse additives (softener)): softener (if desired).

The dispenser is removed by pressing the catch at the top left. Do not exceed the MAX mark. Liquid softening and starching additives are poured into the compartment with the flower symbol before the programme starts. Liquid detergent is poured into the main wash compartment just before starting. With concentrated and liquid detergents choose a programme without a prewash.

## Turn the programme knob to C

Turn the programme knob to **C**: "Short cycle: lightly soiled laundry, 30-40 °C". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Short cycle: main wash, 4 rinses and spin. For lightly soiled laundry.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Switch on with the I/O button

Leave the **1/2 (half load)**, **spin off**, **door** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **I/O**.

On/Off. Press to start the machine, the "Programme in progress" lamp lights. To add laundry while it is running, release the button; press again and the programme continues. Release the button when the wash is finished.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 22 min (manual: 1 h 30 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. The door unlocks about 1-2 minutes after the programme ends.
