Italian machine with a selector of 16 numbered programs and a separate thermostat knob. The program number does not tell you the fabric: the laundry type and temperature can only be read from the table, and the three panel variants have different sets of buttons.

> [!NOTE]
> **The data for this machine is incomplete.** The manual found lacks some information, so the time and some panel marks are approximate. What exactly is missing is listed above the panel drawing.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday cotton clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (main wash): powder.
- the flower mark (fabric softener, fragrance, additives, bleach): fabric softener (optional).

Drawer with three compartments: 1 prewash, 2 main wash, flower for fabric softener, fragrances, additives and bleaches. The left-to-right order is taken from the pictograms in the program table. For hard water use an anti-scale agent together with the powder in compartment 2. Use low-foaming powder for automatic machines. Take the drawer out completely from time to time and wash it under running water.

## Turn the programme knob to 5

Turn the programme knob **P** to **5**: "Cotton, mixed delicate fabrics, lightly soiled items". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Program 5: main wash, rinse, softener, spin.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Switch on with the on/off button

Leave the **stop with water**, **spin off**, **economy**, **extra rinse**, **550/MAX** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **on/off**.

Switches the machine on (on some versions, pull the selector knob towards you instead). The off position is needed to change the program, temperature and buttons. The indicator light comes on {{led red}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 19 min. The machine stops by itself and drains.
