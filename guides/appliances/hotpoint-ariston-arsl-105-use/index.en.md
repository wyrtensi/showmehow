Hotpoint-Ariston with a programme knob of 12 numbers and four letters, separate temperature and spin knobs, four function buttons with LEDs and a Start/Pause button. There is no display; a column of LEDs shows the progress of the cycle.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (compartment 2: main wash detergent (powder or liquid), added before the start): powder or gel.
- **3** (compartment 3: additives (rinse aid/softener), not above the grille): softener if desired.

Bleaching only with programmes 2, 3, 7: bleach goes into insert 4 (placed in compartment 1), detergent and softener into their own compartments. A prewash is not possible with the insert in place. Recommended for very dirty cotton laundry.

## Select programme 3

Turn the programme knob to **3**: "Cotton coloureds (3), lightly soiled whites and delicate coloureds". Lightly soiled whites and delicate coloureds, 89 minutes.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Set the spin to 1000 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **1000** rpm.

## Press Start/Pause

Leave the **Delay timer**, **Easy iron**, **Extra rinse**, **Super wash** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Switch the machine on with **ON/OFF** and press **Start/Pause**.

Starts the programme (the indicator lights green) or pauses it (blinks orange). Press again to resume. A pause is also needed to open the door or cancel a cycle.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 29 min (manual: 1 h 29 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains.
