A German machine with a lettered rotary programme selector (A, B, C, D, F, G, H, J, P) and a separate temperature switch that also serves as the power switch (position O). The programme name is not written on the panel; the key to it is only in the programme table, which is missing from the available copy of the manual.

> [!NOTE]
> **The data for this machine is incomplete.** The manual found lacks some information, so the time and some panel marks are approximate. What exactly is missing is listed above the panel drawing.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday coloureds, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash (the insert for liquid detergent also goes here)): powder.
- the flower mark (softeners for the rinse ("max" mark, do not fill above it)): softener (if desired).

A pull-out drawer with compartments. From left to right in the picture: II main wash, flower for softener, I prewash. The drawer has a blue insert for liquid detergents (placed in compartment II and put away in the spare slot when using powder) and a water hardness reminder disc: use a coin to turn the disc so that the arrow matches the water hardness in your area. The drawer itself is pulled out towards you as far as it goes, lifted and taken out.

## Turn the programme knob to B

Turn the programme knob **A-P** to **B**: "Whites and coloureds, without prewash". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Programme B without prewash.

## Switch on with the temperature switch

Leave the **rpm (spin)**, **e (economy)**, **short programme**, **water level**, **gentle mode** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them.

The temperature switch is also the power switch: in position **O** the machine is off. Turn it to **40**: the machine switches on and runs the programme set on the programme knob. So set the programme before the temperature. The indicator light comes on {{led green}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 8 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. The time is approximate: the programme table is missing from the copy of the manual found.
