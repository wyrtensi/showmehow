Gorenje electronic-control washer with one programme knob (turns both ways, 0 = off), a column of three buttons (spin reduction, quick wash, more water), a Start/Pause button and three phase lamps. The knob does not move during the wash. The manual covers several model variants, so loads and spin speeds are partial.

> [!NOTE]
> **The data for this machine is incomplete.** The manual found lacks some information, so the time and some panel marks are approximate. What exactly is missing is listed above the panel drawing.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday cotton clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 6 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash): powder or gel.
- the flower mark (laundry care products (softener, up to the mark only)): softener (optional).

A drawer with three compartments, left to right in the drawing: prewash (symbol with drop and dot), main wash, laundry care products (flower; fill only up to the mark). If the programme has no prewash, powder can be put directly into the drum with a dosing cup. Dilute thick softeners with water. Some models have a flap: powder up, liquid detergents down (only for programmes without prewash). Keep the drawer closed when opening or closing the door. Remove it by pressing the tab.

## Select programme 40 (EN)

Turn the programme knob to **40 (EN)**: "Cotton 40 °C (EN 60456 test)". Cotton 40 °C (EN 60456 standard), up to 6 kg.

## Set the spin to 1400 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **1400** rpm.

## Press START/PAUSE

Leave the **Quick wash (button C)**, **More water (button D)** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **START/PAUSE**.

Button E: START starts the programme (the machine begins after a few seconds), PAUSE stops it; press START to continue. Until START is pressed the selection can be changed. After START the programme can be changed only by setting the knob to 0 for 1 second.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 2 h 31 min (manual: 2 h 31 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains.
