Dust on the filter blocks the air flow and the air conditioner cools worse. LG advises cleaning the indoor unit filter once every two weeks, or more often if needed. The job is simple, but you have to switch the unit off and let the filter dry completely.

The procedure depends on the panel type: on some models the panel opens at the front, on others at the top. If your unit looks different, check the manual for your model.

> [!WARNING]
> Before you take the filter out, switch the air conditioner off and unplug it. Do not touch the metal parts inside the unit and do not bend the filter: it can break.

## Switch off and unplug

![The plug is pulled out of the socket](step-1.svg)

Turn the air conditioner off with the remote and pull the plug out of the socket. If the unit is mounted high, put a sturdy stool or a ladder next to it.

## Open the front panel

![The front panel of the indoor unit is lifted and the filter mesh is visible](step-2.svg)

Push the front panel of the indoor unit up. If your model has the air intake on top, push the top panel up instead.

## Take the filter out

![The filter is slid out of the unit and held flat](step-3.svg)

Carefully pull the filter out. Hold it flat: it can break if it is bent. When it is fitted correctly, no dust can get into the unit.

## Remove the dust

![A vacuum cleaner nozzle removes dust from the filter mesh](step-4.svg)

If the filter is only lightly dusty, go over it with a vacuum cleaner or a soft brush.

## Wash it if it is very dirty

![The filter in water with detergent and a thermometer with a mark for the maximum temperature](step-5.svg)

If the filter is badly soiled, wash it in water with a neutral detergent. The water must be **no hotter than 40 degrees Celsius**. Do not use volatile substances.

## Dry the filter in the shade

![The filter drying in the shade, the sun is crossed out](step-6.svg)

Put the filter in a well ventilated shady place and let it dry completely. Direct sunlight is bad for it. Do not put a wet filter back.

## Put the filter back and close the panel

Put the dry filter back in place and close the panel. Make sure it is fitted correctly so that dust cannot get into the unit. Then you can plug the air conditioner in.

LG advises cleaning the filter once every two weeks.
