Machine with electromechanical control: two knobs (temperature and program A-M), buttons for On/Off, reduced spin, reduced load and drain cancel. One manual shared with the EW 814 F (850 rpm).

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 4.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (powder for the main wash): powder.
- the flower mark (rinse additives): fabric softener (optional).

The dispenser is removed by pressing the catch at the top left. Pour rinse additives into the compartment with the flower symbol, without exceeding the mark. If you need a prewash, put part of the powder into the compartment with one bar.

## Turn the programme knob to C

Turn the programme knob to **C**: "Short cycle: lightly soiled coloureds, 30-40 °C". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Short cycle: wash, 3 rinses and spin.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Switch on with the I/O button

Leave the **spin**, **1/2 (half load)**, **drain off**, **door** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **I/O**.

On/Off. Press to start the machine: the "Program running" lamp lights up. To add laundry, release the button; pressing it again resumes the program. When the wash has finished, release the button.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 25 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. The door opens about 1-2 minutes after the program ends; while the lock lamp is lit it cannot be opened.
