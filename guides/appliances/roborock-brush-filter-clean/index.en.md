Hair and fur wind around the brushes and dust clogs the filter, so suction drops. This guide shows the main jobs from the Roborock S5 Max manual. The S5 and S6 are almost the same.

The work takes about fifteen minutes, but the filter needs a day to dry after washing. So start in the evening.

## Turn the robot over

![Robot vacuum from below: main brush, side brush, wheels and charging contacts](step-1.svg)

Turn the robot over, wheels up, and put it on a level surface. You will see the main brush, the side brush and the wheels.

## Open the main brush cover and take the brush out

![Robot from below, the cover latch and the main brush are highlighted, an arrow pulls the brush out](step-2.svg)

Unlatch and remove the main brush cover, then take out the brush.

## Remove the bearing and the cap of the brush

Take off the brush bearing and clean it. Then turn the cap at the end of the brush towards unlocking (**Unlock**, the direction is shown on the brush itself) and remove it.

## Cut off the wound-up hair

![Main brush with hair wound around it and a cleaning tool cutting the hair](step-4.svg)

Pull the hair out of the bristles and cut off what is wound tightly with the cleaning tool from the box. If there is a lot of hair, be careful: the brush is easy to damage.

## Put the cap and the brush back

Fit the cap and the bearing, turning the cap towards locking (**Lock**). Put the brush back and replace the cover.

## Unscrew, clean and refit the side brush

![Side brush with three arms and a centre screw, an arrow shows unscrewing](step-6.svg)

Undo the screw that holds the side brush, take the brush off and remove the tangled hair. Put it back and tighten the screw firmly.

## Take out the dustbin and empty it

![Robot from above with the top cover open and the dustbin lifted out](step-7.svg)

Lift the top cover of the robot, squeeze the dustbin latch and take the dustbin out. Open the dustbin lid and pour out the contents. It is best to do this once a week.

## Rinse the filter with clean water

![Pleated filter under running water, water drops falling down](step-8.svg)

Pour clean water into the dustbin, close the cover, shake it gently and pour out the dirty water. Then take out the filter and rinse it with clean water only, without cleaning liquid. Rinse it several times and tap the filter frame to knock out the remaining dirt. Do not touch the filter surface with hands, brushes or sharp objects. The washable filter is cleaned every two weeks.

## Let the filter dry and put it back

Let the filter dry completely. Roborock recommends 24 hours of drying. Then put the filter back, insert the dustbin and close the top cover.

## Keep to the cleaning and replacement schedule

| Part | Clean | Replace |
|---|---|---|
| Main brush | weekly | every 6-12 months |
| Dustbin | weekly | - |
| Washable filter | every two weeks | if the error stays after drying |
| Side brush | monthly | every 3-6 months |
| Sensors and charging contacts | monthly with a soft dry cloth | - |
