A Russian automatic machine with a single programmer knob for 16 programmes and three keys. The programme is chosen with the knob pushed in and started by pulling the knob out towards you; during operation the knob turns by itself to zero.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Coloured laundry, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 4 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put powder in the centre tray

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses the centre tray **b**: main wash, about 110 g of low-foaming powder. Bleach, if you use it, goes in the same tray together with the powder.

The dispenser hopper slides out towards you. The trays must be dry before powder is put in. Special-treatment product is poured in as a solution; do not push the hopper in sharply when fitting it: the solution may spill. After washing, rinse the hopper under running water and wipe it.

## Turn the programme knob to 6

The knob must be pushed in. Turn it clockwise so that **6** lines up with the pointer: "Durable running fabrics, medium soiling". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Programme 6: wash, 5 rinses, spin.

## Pull the knob towards you

Leave the **E**, **Eco**, **No spin** keys released: an ordinary wash does not need them.

Pull the programme knob towards you until it clicks: the wash starts. The indicator light comes on {{led red}}. During the cycle the disc turns clockwise to zero by itself. To stop the machine, push the knob in.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 58 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. After the end, open the door no sooner than 3-4 minutes later: some machines have a hatch lock.
