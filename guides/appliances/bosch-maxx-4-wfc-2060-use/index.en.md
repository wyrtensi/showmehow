German machine with a single program knob where the temperature is built into the knob positions (30, 40, 60, 90) and shown by symbols, plus a separate spin speed selector. Control is electronic: the knob does not turn during the wash, and the program is started with the "Start" button. The panel is confusing because the same numbers appear next to different symbols.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday cotton clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 3.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash, water softening, bleaching, stain removal): powder or gel.
- the flower mark (fabric softener, shaping (up to the mark on insert 1)): fabric softener (optional).

Drawer with three compartments (from left to right in the picture: II, flower, I). Compartment I is for prewash, starching and soaking; compartment II is for the main wash, water softening, bleaching and stain removal; the compartment with the flower is for fabric softener and shaping products. Pour softener no higher than the lower edge of insert 1; dilute thick products slightly with water before loading. To clean, pull the drawer out to the stop, press the recess in the insert and take it out completely.

## Select programme 40

Turn the programme knob to **40**: "Coloureds 40 °C". Coloureds 40 °C, up to 3.5 kg.

## Set the spin to 1000 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **1000** rpm.

## Press Start

Leave the **Anti-crease**, **Extra rinse**, **Quick wash** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **Start**.

"Start" button (diamond): starts the selected program. The "Wash" lamp lights up. The indicator light comes on {{led red}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 2 h (manual: 2 h at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. The door does not open at once: wait about 2 minutes, or if "no spin" is selected and water remains in the tub, choose "Drain" or "Spin".
