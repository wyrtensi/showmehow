A machine that is switched on by pulling out the programme knob: there is no separate power button. Three knobs (spin, temperature, programme A-P) and two buttons (drain off and reduced load). The same manual covers the FA 1032 (1000 rpm).

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 4.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash powder): powder.
- the flower mark (rinse additives): softener (if desired).

The dispenser is removed by pressing the upper catch on the left. Prewash powder goes into the first compartment, main wash powder into the second, rinse additives into the compartment with the flower symbol. When using concentrated powders and liquid detergents, choose a programme without a prewash.

## Turn the programme knob to C

Turn the programme knob to **C**: "Short cycle: lightly soiled laundry, 30-40 °C". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Short cycle: wash, 3 rinses and spin.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Set the spin to 800 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **800** rpm.

## Pull the programme knob towards you

Leave the **drain off**, **1/2 (half load)** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them.

There is no separate power button: the machine switches on when the programme knob is pulled towards you. Pull it and the wash starts. Pushing the knob in stops the machine and cuts the power.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 25 min. The machine stops by itself and drains.
