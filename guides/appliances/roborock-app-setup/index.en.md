This guide follows the official manual for the Roborock Q5. On other Roborock models the buttons and app names may differ a little, but the steps are the same: power, Wi-Fi reset, add the device and the first cleaning run.

The app is updated from time to time, so your screen may look different. Follow the prompts in the app.

## Put the dock against a wall

![The dock stands against a wall with free space on both sides and in front](step-1.svg)

Put the charging dock on a level floor flat against a wall. Plug in the power cable and clear cables off the floor. Leave at least 0.5 m free on each side and more than 1.5 m in front of the dock. Keep it out of direct sunlight and do not cover the beacon, or the robot will not find it. Choose a spot with good Wi-Fi.

## Switch on and put the robot on the dock

![The robot with its power button highlighted, the dock on the right](step-2.svg)

Press and hold the power button on the robot until the light comes on. Put the robot on the dock to charge. If the battery is very low the robot may not switch on: then just place it on the dock.

## Connect your phone to a 2.4 GHz network

The robot works only with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Connect your phone to your home 2.4 GHz network: {{Settings > Wi-Fi}}. If your router uses one name for both bands, temporarily use a separate 2.4 GHz network.

## Install the Roborock app

Find the **Roborock** app in the App Store or Google Play, install it and sign in. For the Roborock Q5 the Mi Home app works too: pick whichever suits you.

## Reset Wi-Fi on the robot

![Dock and power buttons held together on the robot, the Wi-Fi light blinking](step-5.svg)

Press and hold the dock and power buttons together until you hear the voice message that Wi-Fi is being reset. The reset is complete when the Wi-Fi light blinks blue slowly {{led blue blink}}: the robot is waiting for a connection.

## Add the robot in the app

![In the Roborock app the plus button in the top right corner is pressed](step-6.svg)

Open the Roborock app and tap the button to search for a device. In Mi Home tap **+** in the top right corner. Pick your robot and follow the prompts on screen: enter the password of your 2.4 GHz network.

## Wait for the connection

While the robot connects, the Wi-Fi light blinks blue quickly {{led blue fast}}. When it has connected, the light stays blue for about five seconds {{led blue solid}} and the robot appears in the app.

## Start the first cleaning

![The robot drives through the rooms, covering each zone in turn](step-8.svg)

Press the power button on the robot to start cleaning. Start it from the dock and do not move the dock while it works. The robot scans the room, splits it into zones and cleans them one by one. Follow it on the first run: it may get stuck, and this way you spot the problem places right away.

## Look at the map in the app

Open the cleaning map in the app. There you can see the route, split the map into zones and rooms, set no-go zones and invisible walls, and start cleaning by room. If the robot finishes in under 10 minutes, it cleans that area twice.
