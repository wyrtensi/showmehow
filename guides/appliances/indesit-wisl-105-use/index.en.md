A late Indesit without a display: a programme knob with numbers 1-13 and special positions, separate temperature and spin knobs, four unlabelled function buttons and a separate Start/Reset button.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (compartment 2: washing detergent (powder or liquid)): powder or gel.
- **3** (compartment 3: additives (softener, fragrance), not above the grille): softener if desired.

The drawer slides out and is removed by lifting it. Insert 4 for bleach goes into compartment 1, fill no higher than max; a prewash is not possible with the insert in place. Liquid washing detergent is poured into compartment 2 just before the start.

## Select programme 3

Turn the programme knob to **3**: "Lightly soiled whites and coloureds that run". Lightly soiled whites and coloureds, 70 minutes per the table.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Set the spin to 800 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **800** rpm.

## Press Start/Reset

Leave the **Delay**, **Stain removal**, **Easy iron**, **Extra rinse** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Switch the machine on with **I/O** and press **Start/Reset**.

Start/Reset: starts the programme. To cancel a programming mistake, hold it pressed for 2 seconds. The indicator light comes on {{led green}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 10 min (manual: 1 h 10 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. The door opens only after the "Power/Door locked" indicator starts to blink; after a delay the door cannot be opened.
