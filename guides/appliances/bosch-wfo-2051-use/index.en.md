German machine with electronic control: one knob switches the machine on and selects the programme (all printed in German), a second knob sets the spin speed. It starts with a separate Start button and the knobs do not turn during the wash.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 6 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash, water softener, soaking, bleach, stain remover): powder or gel.
- the flower mark (fabric softener, shape-retaining product (up to the mark on insert 1)): fabric softener (optional).

A drawer with three compartments. Compartment I: prewash detergent or starch. Compartment II: main wash, water softener, soaking, bleach or stain remover. Flower compartment: fabric softener or anti-crease product (fill no higher than the lower edge of insert 1). The order of the compartments left to right is taken from the drawing in the manual and matches the related Bosch Maxx 4. Dilute thick or viscous products with water. To clean the drawer, pull it out fully, press on the insert and remove it completely.

## Select programme 40

Turn the programme knob to **40**: "Buntwäsche 40 °C (coloureds, cotton, linen)". Buntwäsche 40, up to 6 kg. Then press Start.

## Set the spin to 1000 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **1000** rpm.

## Press Start

Leave the **Iron (loosening spin)**, **Extra rinse**, **Shirt (intensive wash)**, **Start in (delayed start)** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **Start (diamond)**.

The Start button (diamond) starts the selected programme. The "wash" lamp lights. Press it after choosing the programme and any extra functions.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 2 h 10 min (manual: 2 h 10 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains.
