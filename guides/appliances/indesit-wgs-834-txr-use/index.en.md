A narrow front-loading machine with a mechanical programmer: three oval buttons on the left and three knobs on the right, programmes 1-18 without names, only numbers.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 3.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (main wash): powder or gel.
- the flower mark (fabric softener): softener if desired.

The drawer pulls out towards you with a little effort. The right-hand insert with compartments 3 and 4 lifts out upwards: thickened softener builds up under it, so it is worth rinsing.

## Turn the programme knob to 4

Turn the programme knob **P** to **4**: "Cotton, lightly soiled, short wash". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Turn it while the machine is switched off with the **I/O** button. Short path: wash at step 4, rinses 5-6, spin 7. Suitable for items worn once.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Set the spin to 600 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **600** rpm.

## Switch on with the I/O button

Leave the **1/2 (half load)**, **rinse hold** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **I/O**.

Power button. The programme starts immediately after pressing, so set the knobs first. The indicator light comes on {{led red}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 52 min. The machine stops by itself and drains. The door opens only 2-3 minutes after switching off.
