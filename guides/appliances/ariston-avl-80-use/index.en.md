Slim Ariston front-loader with electronic control: a push-to-release programme knob, separate temperature and spin knobs, four function keys with LEDs and a separate Start/Reset key. No display; the programme knob does not move during the cycle.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (compartment 2: main wash detergent (powder or liquid), poured in before starting): powder or liquid detergent.
- **3** (compartment 3: additives (softeners, fragrances), not above the grille): softener if you like.

The drawer is on the left of the panel and pulls out towards you. Pour liquid wash detergent into compartment 2 right before starting. Do not fill softener in compartment 3 above the grille. To remove it for washing, lift and pull the drawer towards you and rinse it under running water.

## Select programme 4

Turn the programme knob to **4**: "Cotton, lightly soiled whites and coloureds that run (shirts, T-shirts), 40 C". Cotton 40 C, 72 minutes per the table.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Set the spin to 800 rpm

Turn the spin knob to **800** rpm.

## Press Start/Reset

Leave the **Delay timer**, **Super wash**, **Quick wash**, **Extra rinse** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Switch the machine on with **I/O** and press **Start/Reset**.

Starts the programme. To cancel, hold the button for 2 seconds. When a water-stop is chosen, the same button finishes the cycle.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 12 min (manual: 1 h 12 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. Open the lid only when the On/Lid locked indicator flashes again; a steady light means the lid is locked.
