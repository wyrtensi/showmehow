Moisture and detergent residue leave deposits, a smell and mould in the drum and on the rubber door seal. The self-clean programme heats the water to 60-70 °C and removes them without cleaning agents.

The programme runs on its own and the display shows the time left. Its length depends on the model. Your own work takes 10-20 minutes.

## Take the laundry out of the drum

![Washing machine with the door open and an empty drum](step-1.svg)

Open the door and make sure there is no laundry in the drum. Never run the cleaning programme with laundry inside: it may be damaged. Do not add detergent, gel or softener.

## Close the door all the way

Close the door fully, it must be shut tight before the start. Also make sure the machine is plugged in.

## Switch the machine on with Power

Press <kbd>Power</kbd>. If the panel shows an error message, clear it first.

## Select the Drum Clean programme

![Washing machine control panel, the programme dial is set to the drum cleaning position](step-4.svg)

Turn the programme selector (on some models it is called Cycle Selector or Navigation dial) to **Drum Clean+**, **Drum Clean** or **ECO DRUM CLEAN**. The name depends on the model.

## Start the cleaning with Start/Pause

![Washing machine control panel with the Start/Pause button highlighted](step-5.svg)

On many models (**Drum Clean+**, **ECO DRUM CLEAN+**) you need to press and hold <kbd>Start/Pause</kbd>. On models with plain **ECO DRUM CLEAN** a press is enough. If the machine did not start, hold the button. The machine heats the water to 60-70 °C and flushes the drum.

## Wait until the programme finishes

While the programme runs there is nothing to do. When it ends, the display may keep showing Drum Clean complete, this does not get in the way: the machine washes as usual.

## Wipe the rubber seal and leave the door open

![Close-up of the rubber door seal pulled back, wiped with a cloth underneath](step-7.svg)

Pull back the grey rubber seal between the door and the drum and check for stains or foreign objects underneath. Wipe it with a soft brush, a kitchen towel or a damp cloth. Dry it and leave the door open until everything is dry.
