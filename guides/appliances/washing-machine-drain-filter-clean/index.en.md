The drain pump filter catches coins, buttons and lint that end up in the drum with the laundry. When it clogs, the machine may drain badly. Cleaning takes about twenty minutes and needs no special tools, only a screwdriver on some models.

The place of the cover and the lower panel depends on the model: Bosch has a service flap, Indesit has a lower panel held by clips. It is worth checking your own manual, but the order is the same.

> [!WARNING]
> Disconnect the machine from the mains before you start. If you have just run a hot wash, the water in the pump may be hot: wait.

## Unplug the machine

![The plug is pulled out of the socket](step-1.svg)

Make sure the wash cycle has finished and pull the plug out of the socket.

## Open the service flap

![The service flap at the bottom of the front panel of the washing machine is open](step-2.svg)

Open the service flap at the bottom of the front panel. If there is no flap, remove the lower panel on the front: on the Indesit IWC 6103 it comes off with a screwdriver.

## Put a towel down and drain the water

![A towel and a shallow container under the flap, water runs out of the hose](step-3.svg)

Lay a towel under the opening and put a shallow container there. Let the leftover water run out of the drain tube, and only then unscrew the cover.

## Unscrew the pump cover

![The round pump cover is turned counterclockwise](step-4.svg)

Turn the pump cover **counterclockwise** and unscrew it. A little water will come out, which is normal.

## Take everything out of the chamber in front of the pump

![A coin, a button and a lump of lint are taken out of the chamber in front of the pump](step-5.svg)

Carefully check the chamber in front of the pump. Remove coins, buttons and other objects, then clean the inside thoroughly.

## Check the impeller

![The pump impeller is turned by hand](step-6.svg)

Try to turn the impeller by hand. If it does not turn, something is stuck inside: do not use force, contact the manufacturer's customer service.

## Screw the cover back and close the panel

Screw the pump cover back on. Put the lower panel back and make sure it is firmly hooked in, or close the service flap. Then you can switch the machine on.
