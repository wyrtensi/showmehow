Narrow Indesit top-loader with electronic control: programme and temperature knobs, four function buttons with LEDs, a separate Start/Cancel key and no display. The programme knob does not move during the cycle.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the lid, then the drum flaps. Put the laundry in, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the flaps until they click and lower the lid.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The compartments are under the lid. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (compartment 2: main wash detergent (powder)): powder.
- **3** (compartment 3: additives (softener), not above the max mark): softener if you like.

The drawer sits inside the outer lid at the top. The manual says to put liquid detergent straight into the drum in the round plastic dosing ball. Water left in compartment 3 after the wash is normal: it is needed to feed thick softeners. To clean: lightly press the large button on the drawer and pull it up; take out and rinse the pair of siphons in compartments 1 and 2.

## Select programme 4

Turn the programme knob to **4**: "Cotton, lightly soiled whites and coloureds that run (shirts, T-shirts), 40 C". Cotton 40 C, 78 minutes per the table.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Press Start/Cancel

Leave the **Stain removal**, **Extra rinse**, **Spin speed reduction**, **Anti-crease function** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Switch the machine on with **I/O** and press **Start/Cancel**.

Starts the chosen programme. To cancel, hold the button for 2 seconds.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 18 min (manual: 1 h 18 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. Open the lid only when the On/Lid locked indicator flashes again; a steady light means the lid is locked.
