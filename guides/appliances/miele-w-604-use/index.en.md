Miele Novotronic top loader with electronic control: the programme selector turns both ways but does not move while the programme runs, options are set with two buttons plus a Spin button, and the drum parks with its opening up. The temperature is built into each position of the selector.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the lid, then the drum flaps. Put the laundry in, no more than 5.5 kg of dry laundry. Close the flaps until they click and lower the lid.

## Put detergent in compartment II

The compartments are under the lid. This wash uses these compartments:

- **II** (main wash, including soaking): powder or gel.
- the flower mark (softener, shape-retaining additives, liquid starch (with siphon)): softener (optional).

The drawer slides out and has three compartments: prewash (symbol with one bar), main wash and soaking (symbol with two bars), flower: fabric softener, shape-retaining additives, liquid starch (with a siphon; watch the MAX mark). The order of the compartments left to right is not described in the text and is assumed from the usual Miele layout. Use about 1/4 of the dose for prewash. Clean the siphon thoroughly, especially after liquid starch.

## Select programme 40

Turn the programme knob to **40**: "Cotton/Linen 40 °C". Cotton/Linen 40 °C, up to 5.5 kg, spin 1200 rpm.

## Set the spin to 1200 rpm

Press the **Spin** button until **1200** rpm lights up. The drawing shows it as a knob with marks so that all speeds are visible at once.

## Press Start

Leave the **Short (upper button)**, **Prewash (upper button)**, **Soak (upper button)**, **More water (lower button)**, **Lid** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Switch the machine on with **On/Off** and press **Start**.

The Start button starts the selected programme; its indicator blinks when a programme is selected and ready. A short rattling sound at the start is the drum position being locked.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 42 min. The machine stops by itself and drains.
