You can control the robot from your phone only after it is connected to your home Wi-Fi through the **Xiaomi Home** app (formerly called Mi Home). The button combination for pairing mode differs between models, so it is in a table.

The app itself also changes after updates, so follow the on-screen prompts.

## Charge and switch on the vacuum

![Robot vacuum on its charging station with the indicator lit](step-1.svg)

Put the robot on its charging station and wait until it is charged. For pairing it must be charged and switched on. Place the station and the robot where Wi-Fi is strong.

## Connect your phone to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi

The robot understands only 2.4 GHz networks. If your router broadcasts two networks, pick the 2.4 GHz one. The password must not contain special characters such as @ ¥ %. During setup keep the robot fairly close to the router.

## Install Xiaomi Home and sign in

Install the **Xiaomi Home** app from your app store (also called Mi Home), open it and sign in to your Xiaomi account or create a new one. If the app is already installed, the QR code from the robot's leaflet opens its connection page straight away. Allow the app to use location: without it the app may not find the vacuum.

## Put the robot into pairing mode

![Top of the robot vacuum with two buttons highlighted that are held together](step-4.svg)

Press and hold two buttons at the same time. The combination depends on the model:

| Model | Buttons | How you know it worked |
|---|---|---|
| Mi Robot Vacuum | Power and Recharge | The blue indicator flashes |
| Many models (general Xiaomi advice) | Cleaning and Charging | Voice prompt "Reset Wi-Fi" |
| Xiaomi Robot Vacuum S10T | Power On and Recharge, 3 seconds, with the robot on | A prompt tone |
| Xiaomi Robot Vacuum 6 Max | Recharge and Power/Clean, 7 seconds | Robot Wi-Fi is reset |

If your model has different buttons, look in the paper manual.

## Tap the plus in the app

![Phone screen with the app, the plus button highlighted in the top right corner](step-5.svg)

Open Xiaomi Home and tap **+** in the top right corner. On Android the app scans for nearby devices by itself and shows a notification when it finds the vacuum. If the automatic search does not work, pick **Robot Vacuum** from the list of devices.

## Choose Wi-Fi and enter the password

![Phone screen with a list of Wi-Fi networks, one selected, and a highlighted password field](step-6.svg)

Select your 2.4 GHz home network, enter the password and follow the prompts on the screen. Keep the phone next to the robot, within one metre.

## Wait until the robot connects

![Phone and robot vacuum linked by signal waves](step-7.svg)

Stay near the robot until the app reports that it is connected. If it fails, repeat the Wi-Fi reset on the robot and add it again; if it fails again, check the network and the password.

## Check that the robot appears in the app

Open the main screen of Xiaomi Home: the vacuum should be in the device list and online. Now you can control it from the app.
