Electromechanical Indesit front-loader with two knobs (programme and temperature) and three unlabelled buttons. There is no spin knob: spin can only be switched off with a button, and synthetics and wool stop with water in the drum.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday coloured clothes, 40 C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (compartment 2: main wash detergent (powder or liquid)): powder or liquid detergent.
- **3** (compartment 3: additives (softeners, fragrances), not above the grille): softener if you like.

The drawer pulls out towards you. Liquid detergent goes into compartment 2 right before starting; it is suitable for washes up to 60 C and programmes without prewash. Do not fill compartment 3 above the grille. To wash the drawer, pull it out and leave it under running water for a few minutes.

## Turn the programme knob to 4

Turn the programme knob **A** to **4**: "Cotton and linen, coloureds, 40 C". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Turn it while the machine is switched off with the **F** button. Programme 4: wash at 40 C, rinse, softener, spin; 95 min per the table.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob **B** to **40**.

## Switch on with the F button

Leave the **No-spin**, **Extra economy** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **F**.

Button F (positions I and O): pressing it switches the machine on and starts the chosen programme, pressing it again switches off. Choose the programme and temperature before switching on. After a water-stop, switch the machine off with this button before touching the programme knob. The indicator light comes on {{led amber}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 21 min (manual: 1 h 35 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. After the wash wait about 3 minutes, press F (position O) and only then open the door.
