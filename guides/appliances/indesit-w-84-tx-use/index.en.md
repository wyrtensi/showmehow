Front-loading Indesit with two knobs (programme and temperature) and three buttons without text labels. The spin speed is not chosen with a separate knob, and a programme starts only after the power button is pressed and a 5-second wait.

On the panel below, pick what you are washing or tap the marks around the knobs: the time, warnings and drawer compartments update at once. The steps cover an ordinary "Everyday clothes, 40 °C" wash.

## Load the laundry

Open the door and put the laundry in the drum, no more than 5 kg of dry laundry. Close the door until it clicks.

## Put detergent in compartment 2

The drawer pulls out at the top left. This wash uses these compartments:

- **2** (compartment 2: main wash detergent (powder or liquid)): powder or gel.
- **3** (compartment 3: additives (softener, fragrance), not above the grille): softener if desired.

The drawer slides out towards you. To clean it, lift it slightly, take it out and hold it under running water for a few minutes. Insert 4 for bleach is supplied with the machine and goes into compartment 1; fill no higher than the max mark. Liquid detergent is poured into compartment 2 before switching the machine on. Do not use softener and starch together.

## Turn the programme knob to 4

Turn the programme knob to **4**: "Cotton, lightly soiled whites and delicate coloureds". The knob turns clockwise only: if you overshoot the mark, carry on round to it. Cotton, lightly soiled, 95 minutes per the table. Shirts, vests, delicate coloureds.

## Set the temperature to 40 °C

Turn the temperature knob to **40**.

## Switch on with the I/O button

Leave the **Easy iron**, **Slow spin**, **Stain removal** buttons released: an ordinary wash does not need them. Press **I/O**.

Button G, on/off. Switching the machine off does not cancel the programme that was set. The indicator light comes on {{led green}}.

## Wait for the wash to finish

The wash takes about 1 h 35 min (manual: 1 h 35 min at 40 °C). The machine stops by itself and drains. The door is locked and stays closed for about 3 minutes after the end of the cycle: wait until the lock lamp goes out.
