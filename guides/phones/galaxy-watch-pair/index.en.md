Galaxy Watch works with your phone through the Galaxy Wearable app. You pair them once, and after that the watch shows notifications and syncs by itself. Screens and button names can differ a little by phone and app version.

## Charge and turn on the watch

![Galaxy Watch with the upper Home key highlighted and a hold mark beside it](step-1.svg)

Charge the watch fully. Then press and hold the Home key (it is also the power key) for a few seconds until the watch turns on and asks you to choose a language.

## Turn on Bluetooth on the phone

![Phone screen with the switch turned on and a Bluetooth symbol](step-2.svg)

Open {{Settings > Connections > Bluetooth}} on a Samsung phone or {{Settings > Connected devices}} on another Android and turn Bluetooth on. Without it the phone cannot see the watch.

## Install the Galaxy Wearable app

Open Google Play, search for **Galaxy Wearable** and tap Install. If you already have the app, update it to the latest version.

## Start pairing

![A phone and a watch side by side, a pop-up with a connect button on the phone screen](step-4.svg)

### Samsung phone

Choose a language on the watch and place it near the phone. In the pop-up about a new device tap Connect.

### Any Android phone

Open the Galaxy Wearable app and tap Start. If another device is already connected to the app, open the menu and choose Add new device. Then pick your watch in the list of devices found.

## Compare the code on the watch and the phone

![A phone and a watch showing the same row of dots as a confirmation code, the OK button highlighted on both](step-5.svg)

When the phone finds the watch, a code appears on both screens. Check that it matches and confirm on the phone (Accept), then on the watch (OK).

## Allow access and accept the terms

Review the permissions Galaxy Wearable asks for and tap Continue. Then give the app access to the watch and accept the terms of service.

## Choose what to restore and how you wear the watch

If the app offers to restore data from an earlier watch, pick the backup and the data to restore. Then say how you wear the watch and tap Next.

## Activate the carrier plan or skip it

If your watch has LTE and the app asks about a carrier plan, activate it. For a Wi-Fi model or if you do not want a plan, you can skip this.

## Wait for setup to finish

![A watch with a tick on its face and a phone beside it showing a connected symbol](step-9.svg)

The watch shows its watch face and your device appears in the Galaxy Wearable app. Keep the watch near the phone while the first sync runs.
