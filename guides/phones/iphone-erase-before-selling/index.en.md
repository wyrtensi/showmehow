Order matters: first back up and move your data, then sign out, and only then erase. That way the new owner is not stopped by Activation Lock and your data stays yours.

## Back up and move your data

![Old iPhone sending a backup to the cloud and the data to a new phone](step-1.svg)

Back up the iPhone and move the data to your new device. On iOS 11 or later use Quick Start. If your phone number will change, first add extra trusted phone numbers to your Apple Account so you keep access.

## Unpair Apple Watch

If an Apple Watch is paired with this iPhone, unpair it in the Watch app. If needed, cancel your AppleCare plan or transfer it to the new owner.

## Sign out of your Apple Account

![iPhone settings with the account name at the top and Sign Out highlighted at the bottom](step-2.svg)

Open {{Settings > your name}}, scroll down and tap **Sign Out**. On iOS 26 or later choose "Erase this iPhone" or "Sign Out But Don't Erase" and, if asked, confirm "Sign out of iCloud and Store". On iOS 18 or earlier enter your Apple Account password and tap Turn Off.

## Open Transfer or Reset iPhone

![General settings with the Transfer or Reset iPhone row highlighted](step-3.svg)

Open {{Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone}}.

## Tap Erase All Content and Settings

![Transfer or Reset iPhone screen with Erase All Content and Settings highlighted](step-4.svg)

Choose **Erase All Content and Settings**. This removes Apple Pay cards, photos, contacts, music and apps from the phone; the data in iCloud stays.

## Confirm the erase

![Confirmation dialog with a passcode field and the Erase iPhone button highlighted](step-5.svg)

Enter the device passcode if it asks and tap **Erase iPhone**. If the phone uses an eSIM and you are selling it, choose the option that also erases the eSIM. Erasing also turns off Find My and Activation Lock.

## Remove the old device from your trusted devices

![Account screen with a device list and the old iPhone row highlighted](step-6.svg)

Remove the sold iPhone from the list of trusted devices on your Apple Account. Now you can hand the phone to its new owner.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not delete photos and contacts by hand before signing out of iCloud: they disappear from the cloud as well.
