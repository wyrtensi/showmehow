Quick Start sets up a new iPhone with the help of your old one. The old device is busy for a few minutes, so choose a time when you do not need it. Apps, settings and data can move wirelessly, no computer needed.

For a direct wireless transfer both devices need iOS 12.4 or later; Quick Start itself works with iOS 11 or iPadOS 13.

## Update the old iPhone to the latest iOS

Apple recommends updating both devices to the latest version. On the old one open {{Settings > General > Software Update}} and install the update if there is one.

## Back up the old iPhone

It is best to have a fresh backup before you move. If you are short of iCloud space, Apple gives free temporary storage for up to three weeks from the purchase of the new iPhone: on the old device open {{Settings > General > Transfer or Reset iPhone}}, tap Get Started and follow the prompts.

> [!TIP]
> Find your Apple Account password in advance, and if you use a physical SIM or an eSIM, move it to the new phone.

## Get the old iPhone ready

![Old iPhone screen with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth symbols on and the Home Screen bar at the bottom](step-3.svg)

Turn on Wi-Fi and Bluetooth ({{Settings > Wi-Fi}} and {{Settings > Bluetooth}}), unlock the old device and go to the Home Screen.

## Turn on the new iPhone next to the old one

![Two iPhones side by side, the old one on the left and the new one on the right, with a wireless signal between them](step-4.svg)

Press and hold the side button of the new iPhone until the Apple logo appears. Place it next to the old device.

## Follow the prompts on both screens

When the prompt to set up the new device appears on the old one, follow the instructions on both phones. If the prompt disappears, restart both devices.

## Connect the new iPhone to a network

![New iPhone screen with a large Wi-Fi symbol and a cellular signal scale](step-6.svg)

Pick a Wi-Fi network or the cellular network. If the iPhone asks, activate your plan or eSIM.

## Set up Face ID or Touch ID and a passcode

Follow the prompts to set up face or fingerprint recognition. Then set a six-digit passcode: it is needed for Face ID, Touch ID and Apple Pay. For a four-digit or custom passcode, tap Passcode Options.

## Choose how to move the data

![iPhone screen with two cards: a transfer from the cloud and a direct transfer from one phone to another](step-8.svg)

Choose where the data comes from:

- **Download from iCloud** - apps and data arrive in the background and you can use the new iPhone right away.
- **Transfer directly from the previous device** - you have to wait for the process to finish on both phones.

## Keep both devices close and plugged in

![Two iPhones on charge side by side and a progress bar of the data transfer between them](step-9.svg)

Plug both phones into power and keep them together until the transfer is complete. The time depends on the network and the amount of data. When it is done, the new iPhone shows its Home Screen.
