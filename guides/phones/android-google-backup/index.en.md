A Google backup is what you want before a reset, a repair or buying a new phone. It is stored in your account and encrypted, and some of the data is further encrypted with your screen lock.

The backup covers apps and app data, call history, contacts, device settings, SMS and MMS. Photos and videos are saved separately, through Google Photos.

## Set a PIN, pattern or password

![Phone lock screen with a padlock and four PIN dots above a number keypad](step-1.svg)

Google recommends protecting the backup with a strong screen lock: a PIN, pattern or password rather than a plain swipe or Smart Lock. If your phone uses a swipe now, change it in the phone's security settings.

## Open the Backup section

![Phone settings with the Google row highlighted and a backup section with a cloud below it](step-2.svg)

Open {{Settings > Google > All services}} (on some phones {{Settings > Google services}}). Under Backup and restore, tap Backup.

## Turn on backup

![The backup switch turned on and two ticked data type rows below it](step-3.svg)

Turn on the backup switch. Choose what to back up: Photos & videos, Device data, or both.

## Check the backup account

If you are not signed in yet, the phone will ask you to sign in. To use a different account, tap Account storage, enter your PIN, pattern or password if asked, and sign in to the account you want. To switch between accounts, pick the one you need in the same section.

## Tap Back up now

![The back up now button pressed, with a progress ring around it](step-5.svg)

To avoid waiting for the automatic run, tap Back up now in the same section.

## Check what went into the backup

![List of backup data with a tick next to every item](step-6.svg)

Open {{Settings > Google services > Backup}} and look at the list under Backup details. When the copy is saved, "On" appears under the data types you selected. The first backup can take up to 24 hours.

> [!NOTE]
> Section names depend on the phone and Android version: some show "Google > All services", others "Google services". The idea is the same - find the Backup section.
