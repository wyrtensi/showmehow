Connecting an Apple Watch to an iPhone is called pairing. You do it once: after that the watch gets notifications, Health data and settings from the iPhone. The whole setup takes about a quarter of an hour.

## Get the iPhone ready

![iPhone with a software update badge and Bluetooth and Wi-Fi symbols turned on](step-1.svg)

Update the iPhone to the latest iOS. Make sure Bluetooth is on and the iPhone is connected to Wi-Fi or a cellular network. Newer Apple Watch models need an iPhone 11 or later with a current iOS: if your version is too old, the iPhone says so at the start of setup.

## Turn on the Apple Watch and put it on

![Apple Watch with the side button below the crown highlighted and a hold mark beside it](step-2.svg)

Press and hold the side button until the Apple logo appears, then put the watch on your wrist.

## Hold the watch near an unlocked iPhone

![An iPhone and an Apple Watch side by side, the iPhone screen shows a card with a Continue button](step-3.svg)

Unlock the iPhone and hold it next to the watch. Wait for the message "Use your iPhone to set up this Apple Watch" and tap Continue. If the message does not appear, open the Apple Watch app on the iPhone, tap All Watches, then tap Add Watch.

## Tap Set Up for Myself

If the watch is yours, choose Set Up for Myself. For a family member's watch, choose Set Up for a Family Member and follow the prompts. Keep the watch and iPhone close together until these steps are finished.

## Point the iPhone at the animation on the watch

![The iPhone camera sees the animation on an Apple Watch screen, viewfinder corners highlighted](step-5.svg)

Center the watch face in the iPhone viewfinder and wait for the message that the watch is paired. If you cannot use the camera or it cannot read the animation, tap Pair Apple Watch Manually and follow the prompts.

## Choose how to set up the watch

![iPhone screen with two options: set up as a new Apple Watch or restore from a backup](step-6.svg)

If this is your first Apple Watch, tap Set Up as New Apple Watch. The watch may need a watchOS update. If you have set up another watch with this iPhone, a screen called Make This Your New Apple Watch appears: tap Apps & Data and Settings to see how Express Setup will work, then Continue. To choose by hand, tap Customize Settings and pick a backup of your earlier watch.

> [!NOTE]
> If the iPhone says a software update is available, tap Update Now and wait for it to finish before you continue.

## Pick a wrist and accept the terms

Choose which wrist you wear the watch on and tap Continue. Read the Terms and Conditions and tap Agree.

## Sign in to your Apple Account

When asked, enter your Apple Account password. If you are not asked, sign in later in the Apple Watch app on the iPhone: {{General > Apple Account}}. If Find My is not set up on the iPhone, you will be asked to turn on Activation Lock.

## Set a passcode

On the iPhone tap Create a Passcode or Add a Long Passcode, then enter the new code on the watch. You can skip this with Don't Add Passcode, but you need a passcode for Apple Pay.

## Customize the settings

Choose the text size and whether to use bold text. Then add or check details for Fitness and Health, such as your date of birth, and pick the health notifications you want. On cellular models you can set up cellular now. At the end you can set up Apple Pay.

## Wait for the sync to finish

![An Apple Watch with a watch face next to an iPhone, sync arrows between them](step-11.svg)

When setup is done, the watch shows its watch face. While the devices sync in the background, keep the watch and iPhone close together.
