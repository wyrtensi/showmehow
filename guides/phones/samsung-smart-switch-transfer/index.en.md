Smart Switch is built into new Samsung phones. The easiest way is wireless: the phones can stay on their chargers. Below is a transfer from an old Android phone; iPhone is covered in step 4.

## Prepare both phones

![Old and new phones side by side, both with charged batteries](step-1.svg)

Charge both phones. Install Smart Switch on the old phone if it is not there, and put the phones next to each other.

## Open data transfer on the new Galaxy

![Galaxy settings list with the Accounts and backup row highlighted](step-2.svg)

Open {{Settings > Accounts and backup > Bring data from old device}}.

## Give the phones their roles

![Two phones: Send data on the old one and Receive data on the new one](step-3.svg)

On the new phone tap **Receive data**, on the old phone tap **Send data**.

## Pick the system and the method

![Screen with the choice of wireless or cable transfer, wireless highlighted](step-4.svg)

On the old phone choose its system (Android) and **Wireless**. Open Smart Switch on the old phone and tap **Allow**.

> [!NOTE]
> From an iPhone: on the Galaxy pick iPhone/iPad and "Get data from iCloud instead", sign in to iCloud and enter the verification code. For a cable transfer connect the phones with a USB cable (an adapter may be needed), choose Smart Switch and **Receive data** on the new phone, **Allow** on the old one.

## Choose the data and start

![Data list with checkmarks and the Transfer button at the bottom right](step-5.svg)

Tick what to move: contacts, messages, photos, videos and more. Tap **Transfer** at the bottom right.

## Wait until it finishes

![New phone with a full progress bar and a check mark](step-6.svg)

Do not switch the phones off or unplug the cable. When it is done tap **Go to the Home screen**. Some apps may still be installing.
