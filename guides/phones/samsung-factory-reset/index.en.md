A factory reset removes all apps, data and personal information from the phone. It helps before you sell the phone, or when software problems cannot be fixed another way.

> [!WARNING]
> Your data may be impossible to recover after a reset, so save what you need first. Reset only from the settings menu, as described below. A reset done another way, for example through Recovery, turns on FRP protection and the phone will demand the Google account that was on it.

## Save the data you need

Back up your contacts, photos, messages and apps: personal data may not be recoverable after the reset. A copy in your Google or Samsung account will do.

## Remove the Google account from the phone

If you are giving the phone away or selling it, remove the Google account in the phone's account settings. Samsung says FRP is disabled if the account is removed before the reset. Otherwise the phone asks for that account afterwards.

## Get your Samsung password ready and connect to a network

If you are signed in to a Samsung account, you need its password and a network connection to finish the reset, because the phone has to sign out of the account. Connect to Wi-Fi and keep the password at hand.

## Decrypt the microSD card if it is encrypted

If the phone has an encrypted microSD card, decrypt it first - search for "Decrypt SD card" in Settings. Otherwise the phone may not be able to read the card after the reset.

## Open the Reset section

![Samsung settings list with the general management row highlighted and the reset item next to it](step-5.svg)

Open {{Settings > General management > Reset}}. If there is no General management, the item may be called Backup and reset or Privacy.

## Choose Factory data reset

![Reset screen with the factory data reset row highlighted and a warning symbol](step-6.svg)

Tap Factory data reset (older versions call it Reset data) and read what will be erased.

## Tap Reset, then Delete all

![Bottom of the reset screen, the reset button and then the red delete all button pressed in turn](step-7.svg)

Swipe to and tap Reset, then tap Delete all.

## Confirm the reset with your passwords

![Password entry screen with dots and a confirm button](step-8.svg)

If a screen lock is set, enter it. If you are signed in to a Samsung account, enter its password.

## Wait for the restart

![The phone restarting, a progress indicator on the screen](step-9.svg)

The phone restarts and shows the initial setup screen. If it takes very long to turn on after the reset, wait: booting can take up to an hour, and then contact a Samsung service centre.
