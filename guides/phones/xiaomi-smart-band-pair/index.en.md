Xiaomi Smart Band 8 works through the Mi Fitness app. This guide uses the device search, which is the same on Android and iOS; on iOS you also tap Pair on the phone at the end.

## Install the Mi Fitness app

![Phone home screen with the Mi Fitness icon highlighted](step-1.svg)

Get **Mi Fitness** from Google Play or the App Store. Turn Bluetooth on.

## Sign in with your Xiaomi account

Open the app, sign in with your Xiaomi account (create one if needed) and grant the permissions it asks for.

## Tap plus, then Add devices

![Mi Fitness app with the plus icon at the top right and the Add devices row](step-3.svg)

In the top right corner tap **+**, then **Add devices**. Depending on the app version you can also pick **Add model** and Xiaomi Smart Band 8, or scan the QR code on the band with **Scanner**.

## Pick your band in the list

![Phone with a list of found devices, the band entry highlighted and the band beside it](step-4.svg)

Put the band next to the phone and wait for the search. Choose the device whose Bluetooth address matches your band.

## Confirm on the band

![Band screen with a check mark to tap](step-5.svg)

When the phone has linked to the band, a check mark appears on its screen. Tap it. On iPhone then tap **Pair** on the phone.

> [!NOTE]
> Some Xiaomi phones with MIUI show a "Connect to this device" window by themselves when a new band is near. Then just tap that button.
