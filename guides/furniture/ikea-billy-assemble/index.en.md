BILLY is a tall open bookcase: you build it lying down, then stand it up and anchor it to the wall. The steps below follow the IKEA instructions for the white 80 cm model, in our own words. Other sizes go together in the same order with a different number of parts.

> [!WARNING]
> The bookcase must be fixed to the wall. If it tips over it can cause serious or fatal injury. IKEA states that this furniture has to be used with the wall brackets supplied.

## Prepare the space and check the parts

Clear the floor and lay down cardboard or a blanket. Lay the parts out and check the fittings against the list above. You need a screwdriver, a hammer, a pencil and a ruler. The bookcase is 202 cm tall, so leave enough space above it to fix the brackets near the ceiling.

## Press the dowels into the panel ends

![Panel end with two wooden dowels being pressed into the holes](step-2.svg)

Press 16 wooden dowels into the holes in the ends of the horizontal panels. They should sit tight; tap them lightly with the hammer if needed.

## Screw the cam bolts into the side panels

![Cam bolt being screwed into a side panel with a screwdriver](step-3.svg)

Screw 12 cam bolts into the side panels, six in each, until they stop. They must not wobble.

## Stand the horizontal panels on the first side panel

![Side panel lying flat with three horizontal panels standing on its pins](step-4.svg)

Lay one side panel on the floor with its inner face up. Set the horizontal panels on it, pushing the dowels into the holes. Check the position of every panel against picture 3 in the instructions: it points out the cam lock holes and shows the wrong way round.

## Lock the panels with cam locks

![Cam lock in a panel hole being turned with a screwdriver to pull the joint tight](step-5.svg)

Put the first six cam locks (four and two, as in pictures 4 and 5 of the instructions) into their sockets and turn each one with the screwdriver until the panel is pulled in with no gap.

## Put the second side panel on top

Lower the second side panel onto the dowels and bolts, then lock the remaining six cam locks the same way as in the last step. The frame is now built.

## Mark the middle of the back edges

Turn the frame so its back faces up. Find the middle of the back edges of the horizontal panels and mark it with a pencil: the nails will follow this line.

## Unroll the back panel

![The back panel being unrolled over the rear of the bookcase lying on its front](step-8.svg)

The back panel is thin and comes rolled up. Unroll it over the frame and line it up with the edges so that no side sticks out.

## Draw a line through the marks

Lay the ruler on the marks and draw a line across the back panel with the pencil. The middle row of nails goes along it.

## Nail the back panel on

![Back panel with rows of nails along the edges and the centre line, one nail being hammered in](step-10.svg)

Drive in 18 nails along the edges and the drawn line, starting at the corners. Each nail must go into the edge of a panel, not past it. Then stand the bookcase upright.

## Fix the bookcase to the wall

![Tall bookcase beside a wall, an angle bracket joins its top to the wall](step-11.svg)

Move the bookcase into place. Screw the two brackets to the back of the top panel with the screws supplied, then fix their other arm to the wall with screws and plugs chosen for your wall. These are not in the box.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not skip this step, even if the bookcase is empty for now or stands in a corner.

## Fit the shelf supports and shelves

![Shelf support pressed into the side panel, a shelf resting on four supports](step-12.svg)

Press four shelf supports into holes at the same height on both sides, then lay a shelf on them. In total there are 16 supports and four loose shelves.
