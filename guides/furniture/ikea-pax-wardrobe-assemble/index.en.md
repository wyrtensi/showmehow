A new PAX frame comes folded and goes together standing up: you unfold it, add the feet and the top panel, then move it to the wall. This is the IKEA instructions for the white 100 cm frame in our own words - picture numbers are given so you can check against the original. Doors, hinges and interior fittings are sold separately.

> [!WARNING]
> The wardrobe must be fixed to the wall. IKEA warns that furniture that tips over can cause serious or fatal injury. Screws and plugs for the wall are not in the box - choose ones that suit your wall.

## Check the ceiling height and call a helper

You need two people - IKEA says so. The ceiling must be at least 202 cm to build the frame upright, and at least 205 cm for sliding doors. The frame is 201.2 cm tall, so there is little room to spare. Put a step ladder nearby and take a spirit level.

## Lean the folded frame against the wall

![Folded frame standing on its long edge and leaning against a wall](step-2.svg)

Together, stand the folded frame on its long edge by the wall where the wardrobe will go. One of you holds it while the other steadies it.

## Remove the two straps

![Strap on the folded frame being peeled off by its tab, scissors crossed out](step-3.svg)

Take off the two straps by pulling the tabs on them (picture 2). Do not cut them with scissors - the pictogram in the instructions says so.

## Unfold the frame

![Frame from above: the two folded side panels swing open like a book](step-4.svg)

Open the side panels like a book until the frame is rectangular (pictures 3 and 4). Hold the panels with both of you so they do not fall.

## Fit the feet and the bottom parts

![Adjustable foot snapped onto the bottom corner of a side panel](step-5.svg)

Snap the four adjustable feet onto the bottom corners of the side panels, then add the bottom pieces and clips between them (pictures 5-10).

## Fit the L and R corner pieces

![Top corners of the frame: a left and a right corner piece are pressed into place](step-6.svg)

The top corners take pieces marked L and R - do not swap the sides (pictures 11 and 12). Stand on the step ladder to reach them.

## Hang the top panel

![Step ladder beside the frame and a top panel being hooked in at one end first](step-7.svg)

From the step ladder, hook one end of the top panel in first, then lift the other end (picture 13). Do not push it in at an angle - the crossed-out picture shows that. Then push the corner pieces up until the edges sit flush (picture 14).

## Level the wardrobe

![Hex key turning the adjuster of a foot to raise or lower the wardrobe](step-8.svg)

Put the spirit level on the frame and turn the adjuster in each foot with the hex key until the wardrobe stands level (pictures 16 and 19). Check both vertically and horizontally.

## Move the wardrobe to the wall and fix it

![Top of the frame near a wall: a spacer sleeve reaches the wall across a small gap](step-9.svg)

Wind the spacer sleeves out of the top corners until they touch the wall, with a gap of no more than 22 mm (picture 18). Then fix the frame to the wall with the supplied parts and your own screws and plugs, as pictures 15 and 17-19 show.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> According to the IKEA product page, the set includes fittings for installing with or without a skirting board.

## Click the covers on and press the bottom panel

Close the covers on the corner pieces until they click (picture 20). Then press the bottom panel down so it sits in place (picture 21). The frame is done - you can hang the doors and add the interior fittings.
