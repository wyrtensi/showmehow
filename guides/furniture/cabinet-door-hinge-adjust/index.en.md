  - q: The screw turns but the door does not move
    a: The hinge is probably not clipped properly onto the mounting plate. Open the door, press the back of the hinge arm down with a finger until it clicks, and try again. If the plate wobbles, tighten its screws on the cabinet side.
  - q: The adjustment is not enough, the gap is still wide
    a: Each adjustment has a small range - according to Blum, up to 2 mm sideways, from minus 2 to plus 3 mm in depth and about 2 mm in height. If that is not enough, the door itself or the cabinet body is out of true. Check that the cabinet stands level and the door has not warped.
  - q: The door slams shut without the soft close
    a: On a CLIP top BLUMOTION hinge the soft close can be switched off on one of the hinges with a switch on its body, so that a light door does not bounce back. Move the switch back to its original position and close the door once to bring the soft close back.

Blum CLIP top hinges adjust in three directions: sideways, in and out, and up and down. You only need a screwdriver and you do not have to take the door off. Work out what is wrong first and turn one screw at a time, so you can see what each one does.

## Open the door and find the hinge

![Concealed hinge from above: plate on the cabinet wall, arm with two adjustment screws and the cup in the door](step-1.svg)

Open the door wide. The hinge has three parts: a mounting plate on the cabinet side, an arm in the middle and a cup sunk into the door. The arm carries two adjustment screws, and the plate has its own fixing screws.

> [!NOTE]
> A CLIP hinge comes off without tools: press the lever under the arm. To put it back, set it on the plate and press the back of the arm down with a finger until it clicks.

## Decide what needs adjusting

Look at the closed door and compare it with the table.

| What you see | What to adjust |
|---|---|
| The gap to the next door or the side wall is too wide or too narrow | Sideways (overlay) |
| The door sticks out in front or sits deeper than the cabinet | In and out (depth) |
| The bottom edge is lower than its neighbour's, or the door sags | Up and down (height) |

Change one thing at a time and close the door after every change.

## Move the door sideways with the front screw

![Cabinet from above: the door slides along the front, the gap to the next door changes](step-3.svg)

Turn the front screw on the hinge arm a little at a time, about a quarter turn. According to Blum it changes the overlay by 2 mm either way. If the door moves in and out instead of sideways, you have found the other screw - go back.

## Set the depth with the rear screw

![Cabinet from above: the door moves toward the cabinet body or away from it](step-4.svg)

On CLIP hinges Blum says to loosen the rear screws on all the hinges of this door slightly first. Then turn the rear screw with the spiral cam: the door moves from minus 2 mm to plus 3 mm. When you are done, tighten the screws you loosened.

## Raise or lower the door on the plate

![Cabinet front: the door moves up or down on its two hinges](step-5.svg)

Height is set on the mounting plate, not on the arm. If the plate has a cam, turn it: the door moves roughly 2 mm up or down. If there is no cam, loosen the plate screws, hold the door in the right position and tighten the screws again. Plates of that kind give up to 3 mm.

## Compare the gaps

![Two cabinet doors side by side: an even gap between them and a common line along the bottom edge](step-6.svg)

Close the door and look at the gap along its whole height. It should be the same at the top and the bottom, and the bottom edges of neighbouring doors should line up. If not, go back to the right step and adjust a little more.

## Tighten the screws and test the door

Make sure every screw you loosened is tight again, then open and close the door several times. It should move freely and not catch on the next door or the cabinet. A light door on a BLUMOTION hinge should close smoothly and quietly.
