This is a retelling of the official IKEA instructions for the MALM chest of 3 drawers (version AA-2208395-1). The instructions themselves are only pictures, so words and part numbers from them are added here. The chest can differ a little between countries and colours, so keep the paper instructions from your box close.

You need screwdrivers and a hammer, and the floor should be covered with cardboard or a blanket so the parts do not get scratched. It is easier with two people.

> [!WARNING]
> The chest must be anchored to the wall: an unanchored chest can tip over, especially with drawers pulled out. The last step of this guide covers it, do not skip it.

## Prepare the place and check the parts

Lay cardboard or a blanket on the floor and lay out the parts. Compare the fittings with the bag at the start of the instructions: bolts, dowels, screws and cam locks. Do not assemble the chest on a bare floor.

## Screw the bolts into the base panel

![Four connecting bolts are screwed into the base panel lying on a soft surface](step-2.svg)

Lay the base panel on a soft surface and screw four bolts **118331** into it with a screwdriver.

## Add the dowels and stand the sides up

![Wooden dowels are pushed into the ends of the side panels, then the sides stand on the base](step-3.svg)

Push four dowels **101345** into the end of each side panel and stand the sides on the base. One side panel has a warning label on its inner face.

## Tighten the sides with the cam locks

![A cam lock is turned with a screwdriver to pull the joint tight](step-4.svg)

On each side put four cam locks (article **117434**, **119030** or **120076**, depending on the colour) into the sockets on the bolts and turn each one with a screwdriver until the joint is tight.

## Screw on the cross rail

Fasten rail **10054466** to the side panel with one screw **105344**, as in picture 4 of the instructions.

## Lower the back panel

![The thin back panel is lowered between the side panels](step-6.svg)

Lower the back panel between the sides from above, as in picture 5.

## Fit the top rail and hammer in the bolts

Slide the top rail into the cut-outs as in picture 6. Then put four bolts **110519** in as in picture 7 and hammer them in.

## Fasten the second rail and add the feet

Swing the second rail into place as in picture 8 and fasten it with a screw **105344**. Screw two screws **115339** into the places shown in picture 9.

## Fit the runners to the drawers

![A metal runner is screwed to the side of a drawer](step-9.svg)

Screw the runners to the drawers. They come as left (**DL**) and right (**DR**) and are marked. Fix each runner with two screws **100365**, as in picture 10 of the instructions.

## Slide the drawers in

![The drawers are pushed into the chest, two pulled-out drawers are crossed out beside it](step-10.svg)

Slide the drawers into the chest. Never pull out more than one drawer at a time: the chest could tip over.

## Anchor the chest to the wall

Fasten the chest to the wall with the included restraint, as in the separate leaflet in the box (picture 11 refers to it). Details are in IKEA's guide to [securing furniture](https://www.ikea.com/gb/en/campaigns/secure-it-pub10077d91/) and in our guide on anchoring furniture to the wall.
