Unsecured chests of drawers, bookcases and wardrobes can tip over, and small children are at most risk. A drawer pulled out acts as a lever: even a light child can topple a large chest. Anchoring takes about half an hour.

This guide collects IKEA's general recommendations. For a specific piece, the leaflet in your box stays the main source: it shows where and at what height to fasten.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Anchor every chest of drawers or tall unit, whatever the model. Never rely on furniture being stable just because it looks sturdy.

## Find out what your wall is made of

![Four wall types: a wooden stud behind plasterboard, plasterboard alone, solid masonry and solid wood](step-1.svg)

Work out what the wall is: plasterboard over a wooden stud, plasterboard without a stud, a solid brick or concrete wall, or solid wood. This decides which fixings you need. If you are not sure, ask a professional or a hardware store.

## Choose the fixings

![The tip-over kit: a bracket, a wall plug and a screw, with a strap beside them](step-2.svg)

Take the bracket, strap, screws and plugs from the furniture kit. The included fixings suit most solid and hollow walls. For solid wood use the screws without plugs, for brick, concrete and plasterboard without a stud use a screw with a plug. If you do not have the right fixings, buy them for your wall.

## Screw the bracket to the furniture

![The bracket is screwed to the back panel of the furniture near the top edge](step-3.svg)

Screw the bracket or strap to the back of the furniture as shown in the instructions for that piece.

## Mark the wall and drill the holes

![A drill makes a hole in the wall at the pencil mark](step-4.svg)

Push the furniture against the wall, mark the fixing point with a pencil and move the furniture away. If there is a wooden stud behind the plasterboard, the screw (IKEA's example is 5 mm for wood) goes straight into it. Otherwise you need a plug: IKEA's example is 8 mm, so drill a hole sized for your plug and push the plug in.

## Fasten the bracket to the wall

![A screw goes through the bracket into the plug in the wall and pulls it tight](step-5.svg)

Screw the bracket or strap to the wall: straight into the wood on a stud, into the plug in other cases. Tighten it so that the furniture cannot move away from the wall.

## Test the fixing

![The furniture is pulled forward by its top and stays against the wall, a check mark beside it](step-6.svg)

Pull the furniture forward by its upper part: it must stay at the wall. Keep heavy things in the lower drawers, and do not put a TV or other heavy objects on top.

## Teach the rules at home

Children must not climb or hang on drawers, doors and shelves, and must not open several drawers at once. Adults also should not open more than one drawer or lean on an open drawer to stand up.
