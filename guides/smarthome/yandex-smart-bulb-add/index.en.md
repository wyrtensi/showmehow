  - q: The bulb does not blink after the light is switched on
    a: Switch it on and off five times in a row, leaving it off for about two seconds each time, and wait until it starts blinking white. If it does not, switch it off for ten seconds and repeat.
  - q: The app cannot see the bulb or cannot connect it
    a: Check that the phone is on a 2.4 GHz network, not 5 GHz. If there are two networks, pick the one without 5 or 5G in its name. Then restart the router by unplugging it for ten seconds and fully close and reopen the app.

Yandex bulbs come in two kinds: Wi-Fi and Matter. A Wi-Fi bulb needs only a router and a phone. A Matter bulb also needs a Yandex Station or Hub. You can tell the kind by the QR code with the Matter logo on the bulb or in the paper instructions. The order below is for the Wi-Fi model.

> [!NOTE]
> With a Matter bulb a banner "Bulb found" appears in the app after you switch the light on. Tap "Connect" and scan the QR code. For the first connection it is better to put the Station or Hub close to the bulb.

## Check the phone's Wi-Fi network

The phone must be on a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. The bulb cannot see 5 GHz. If your router has two networks, pick the one without "5" or "5G" in its name. Install the Dom s Alisoj app and sign in with your Yandex account.

## Switch the light off and screw in the bulb

![Bulb being screwed into the socket with the light switched off](step-2.svg)

Switch the room light off so the socket has no power and screw the bulb in all the way.

## Switch the light on

![Lit smart bulb blinking quickly](step-3.svg)

Switch the light on. The bulb starts blinking fast - this means it is ready to connect.

## Tap the plus in the app

![Phone screen with a round plus button in the top right corner](step-4.svg)

In the Dom s Alisoj app tap the plus icon in the top right corner.

## Choose the bulb type

Open {{Smart home device > Yandex > Bulb > Wi-Fi bulb}} and follow the prompts on the screen.

## Pick the network and wait for the connection

![Router with Wi-Fi waves, a phone and a bulb on the same network](step-6.svg)

Choose your home 2.4 GHz network, enter its password and wait while the app finds the bulb and adds it.

## Test the control

Switch the bulb on and off in the app. If it responds, the connection is done. After that you can name it, put it in a room and control it by voice through Alice.
