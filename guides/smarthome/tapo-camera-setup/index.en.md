Tapo cameras are added through the Tapo app. From power-on to the first picture in the app takes about five minutes.

The camera works only on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. If your router has two bands, make sure 2.4 GHz is on.

## Install the Tapo app and sign in

Install the Tapo app from your app store. Sign in with a TP-Link ID or create one.

## Get your network and password ready

Find out the name of your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and its password: you need them at the end of setup.

## Power the camera and wait for red and green blinking

![A camera with its status light blinking red and green](step-3.svg)

Connect the camera to power and wait about 30 seconds ({{timer 30s}}). The light should blink red and green ({{led red/green alternate}}): this means the camera is ready to pair.

## Reset the camera if the light blinks differently

![The back of the camera with the small reset button highlighted and a timer ring](step-4.svg)

If there is no red and green blinking, hold the reset button for at least 5 seconds ({{timer 5s}}) and check the light again.

## Add the camera in the app

![App screen with a list of device types and the Indoor Cameras row highlighted](step-5.svg)

Tap the plus, choose **Add Device**, then the camera type, for example **Indoor Cameras**, and your model. For cameras with an Ethernet port choose the connection method: wired or wireless.

## Connect to the camera

For cameras with Bluetooth, turn it on on the phone. On Android allow location access and tap **Connect**. On iPhone open the Wi-Fi settings, join the network Tapo_Cam_XXXX (XXXX is the last four digits of the camera MAC address) and return to the app.

## Pick your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and enter the password

![A list of networks in the app with the home network selected and a password field below](step-7.svg)

Wait one to two minutes for the app to find the camera. Select your 2.4 GHz network, enter the password and tap **Next**.

## Name the camera and finish

Enter a unique name for the camera, choose its location and tap **Next**. When the Complete screen appears, tap **Done**.
