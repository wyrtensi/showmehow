The Station is set up through the Dom s Alisoy app, and later you control the speaker and your smart home in it. The "Yandex with Alice" app lacks some settings, so use Dom s Alisoy.

You need a Yandex account, and you must sign in to the app with it.

## Place the speaker and plug it in

![A smart speaker with a glowing top ring connected by a cable to a wall outlet](step-1.svg)

Place the Station at least 20-30 cm away from walls: the position strongly affects the sound. Plug the power adapter into a wall outlet.

## Wait for Alice's greeting

When the speaker has booted, Alice says hello. If you set a personal greeting beforehand, she says it when the speaker connects to the internet.

## Install Dom s Alisoy and sign in

Install the Dom s Alisoy app from your app store, open it and sign in to your Yandex account.

## Tap Connect on the banner

![Phone screen with a banner about a found Station and the Connect button highlighted](step-4.svg)

The app shows a banner that a Station was found. Tap **Connect** and follow the instructions on the screen.

## If there is no banner, add the speaker manually

![App screen with the plus icon in the top right corner highlighted and a menu item below](step-5.svg)

Tap the plus in the top right corner, choose **Device with Alice** and select the new speaker in the list. Then follow the prompts in the app and Alice's voice prompts.

## Choose the Wi-Fi network and enter the password

![A phone screen with a list of networks and sound waves going from the phone to the speaker](step-6.svg)

In the app select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password. The app passes it to the speaker. Keep the room quiet while it does so.

> [!WARNING]
> When you set up by sound, the Wi-Fi password is encoded in it. Do not let other people record it.

## Say "Alice" and test the speaker

When setup is finished, say "Alice" and ask any question. If she answers, the speaker works.
