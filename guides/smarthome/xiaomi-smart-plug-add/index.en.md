  - q: The indicator is solid orange, not blinking
    a: On the Smart Plug 2 a solid orange light means the plug failed to connect to the network. Check the password and that the network is 2.4 GHz, then add the plug again. If it stays orange, reset the plug (step 8) and try again.
  - q: The indicator is off
    a: There is no power or the plug is switched off. Check that the plug sits firmly in the wall outlet and that the outlet itself has power.
  - q: The app does not find the plug
    a: Make sure the plug is waiting for a connection (blinking orange), the phone is on the same 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network and you are not using a VPN. Move the phone closer to the plug and update the app.
  - q: How do I share the plug with my family

A Xiaomi smart plug connects to Wi-Fi and is controlled from the Mi Home app (in some regions called Xiaomi Home). Once added, you can switch the plug on and off from the app, by schedule and in smart home scenes.

The plug works only on 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks, so check beforehand that your phone is on such a network.

## Install the app and sign in

Install the Mi Home or Xiaomi Home app from your app store. Open it and sign in to your Xiaomi account or create a new one.

## Plug the smart plug into the mains

Put the plug into a wall outlet where Wi-Fi is stable. The indicator on the body should light up. Avoid damp places.

## Wait for the indicator to blink orange

![A smart plug with its indicator blinking orange](step-3.svg)

On the Smart Plug 2 a blinking orange light means the plug is waiting for a network connection. The older Mi Smart Plug (Wi-Fi) may show its light differently: follow the prompts in the app.

## Tap the plus in the app

![App home screen with the plus button in the top right corner highlighted](step-4.svg)

Open the app and tap the plus in the top right corner. The app scans on its own for plugs that are switched on but not set up yet.

## Pick the plug and the Wi-Fi network

Tap the plug that was found in the list. If it is not there, choose the model manually. Select your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, enter the password and confirm.

## Wait for a solid blue indicator

![A smart plug with a solid light and a phone showing a check mark for a successful connection](step-6.svg)

While the plug connects, the indicator blinks blue. When it finishes, the light stays blue. The app shows a success message.

## Name the plug and test it

Give the plug a clear name, for example after the appliance in it, and choose a room. Tap the plug icon in the app or the button on the body and check that the appliance switches on and off.

## Reset the plug if it will not add

![A smart plug with its button held down and a timer ring next to it](step-8.svg)

Hold the button on the plug body until the indicator starts blinking orange. This returns the plug to factory settings and it waits for a connection again. Then repeat steps 4-6.

> [!NOTE]
> The hold time depends on the model, check the exact value in the manual from the box. The Mi Smart Plug (Wi-Fi) manual only says that a long press restores factory settings.
