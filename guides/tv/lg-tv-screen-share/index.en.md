An LG webOS TV can receive a picture from a phone or a laptop over the air. Pick the part that matches your device: steps 2-5 for Android, steps 6-7 for iPhone, steps 8-10 for a Windows 11 laptop.

## Get the devices ready

Switch on the LG TV. Turn Wi-Fi on on your phone or laptop. For an iPhone, the phone and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network. Before the iPhone steps, check that the TV runs webOS 4.0 (2018) or newer and the iPhone has iOS 12.4 or newer.

## Android: open the notification panel

![A phone with the notification panel pulled down, showing a row of quick settings tiles](step-2.svg)

Swipe down from the top edge of the screen to open the notification panel.

## Android: tap Screen Share

Find **Screen Share** in the panel and tap it. On some phones it has another name, for example Smart View: the exact flow depends on the phone and TV model.

## Android: choose your TV

![A phone screen with a list of external devices, the TV row highlighted in the list](step-4.svg)

Select your TV in the list of external devices.

## Android: accept the request on the TV

![A TV with a request window and two buttons, the Yes button highlighted](step-5.svg)

A connection request appears on the TV. Select **Yes**, and the phone screen shows up on the TV.

## iPhone: open Screen Mirroring

Swipe down to open Control Center and tap **Screen Mirroring**.

## iPhone: choose your TV

Pick the TV in the list of devices. If an AirPlay code appears on the TV, enter it on the iPhone.

## Windows: press Win + K

![Keyboard keys with the Windows logo key and the K key highlighted](step-8.svg)

Switch the TV on and press the Windows logo key and the <kbd>K</kbd> key on the laptop together. A list of displays you can cast to opens.

## Windows: select the LG TV

Select the TV in the list of available devices. If extra prompts appear on the screen, follow them.

## Windows: pick a display mode

![Three pairs of screens: duplicate, extend and second screen only, the first one selected](step-10.svg)

Press <kbd>Win</kbd> + <kbd>P</kbd> and choose a mode: **PC screen only**, **Duplicate**, **Extend** or **Second screen only**.

## Check the picture on the TV

![A TV showing the cast window with icons for moving, sound and full screen](step-11.svg)

Make sure the screen of your device appears on the TV. If the picture is in a window, you can move it, turn the device audio on the TV, or switch it to full screen.
