A Samsung TV needs the internet for apps and updates. Wi-Fi is the easiest way, but you can also plug an Ethernet cable into the LAN port of the TV. Menu names differ between models, below are the paths for most TVs.

## Get your network name and password ready

Take the remote and make sure you know your Wi-Fi network name and its password. They are often printed on a sticker on the router.

## Open the TV settings

![TV home screen with a side menu and the Settings icon highlighted](step-2.svg)

Press **Home** on the remote and choose the gear icon **Settings**.

## Open the network settings

Go to {{Settings > General > Network > Open Network Settings}}. On some models the path is {{Settings > All Settings > Connection > Network}}.

## Pick your Wi-Fi network

![A list of Wi-Fi networks on a TV screen with one network selected and highlighted](step-4.svg)

Choose the Wireless connection type and select the name of your network in the list.

## Enter the password

![An on-screen keyboard with the password entered and the confirm key highlighted](step-5.svg)

Type the password on the on-screen keyboard, choose **Done** and confirm with **OK**. When the connection succeeds, the TV shows a message.

## Check the network status

Open {{Settings > General > Network > Network Status}}. If all is well, you will see a message that the TV is connected to the internet.

## Start the software update

![Software Update screen: the Update Now row is highlighted and an Auto Update switch is below it](step-7.svg)

Open {{Settings > General > Support > Software Update}} and choose **Update Now**. If a new version is found, confirm the installation. The TV restarts by itself.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not unplug the TV or switch it off while the update is installing.

## Turn on Auto Update

On the same screen choose **Auto Update** and turn it on. The TV then downloads updates on its own while it is connected to Wi-Fi.
