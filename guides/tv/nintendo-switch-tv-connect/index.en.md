The dock charges the console and sends the picture to the TV. This guide is written for Nintendo Switch and Switch OLED. Switch 2 follows the same scheme but needs a different AC adapter and HDMI cable, see the warning below.

## Check what you need

You need the console, the dock, the Switch AC adapter (model HAC-002) and an HDMI cable.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Nintendo Switch Lite does not work with a TV. Switch 2 needs its own NGN-01 AC adapter and an Ultra High Speed HDMI cable: the ones from the first Switch will not do.

## Open the back cover of the dock

![Dock seen from behind: the back cover is open and two connectors are visible under it](step-2.svg)

Turn the dock so its back faces you and open the cover over the connectors. There are two connectors inside: the upper one is labelled AC ADAPTER, the lower one HDMI OUT.

## Connect the AC adapter to the dock

![The AC ADAPTER connector on the dock is highlighted, a USB Type-C plug sits in it and the cable goes to a wall outlet](step-3.svg)

Put the USB Type-C plug of the AC adapter into the upper AC ADAPTER connector and plug the adapter into a wall outlet.

## Connect the HDMI cable to the dock and the TV

![The HDMI OUT connector on the dock is highlighted, a cable runs from it to the TV](step-4.svg)

Put one end of the HDMI cable into the lower HDMI OUT connector on the dock and the other end into a free HDMI input of the TV or monitor.

## Close the back cover

Put the cover back and make sure it is closed.

## Remove the Joy-Con from the console

Slide the left and right Joy-Con up and off the console.

> [!NOTE]
> If the Joy-Con were not attached to the console before, register them on it first, otherwise they will not work as wireless controllers.

## Put the console into the dock

![The console is lowered into the dock with its screen facing the same way as the dock front, two Joy-Con lie apart on the sides](step-7.svg)

Turn the console so its screen faces the same way as the front panel of the dock and insert it into the dock. The console screen will go dark, which is normal.

## Switch on the TV and select the HDMI input

![A TV with the right HDMI input highlighted in the input list and the Switch picture on the screen](step-8.svg)

Turn the TV on and pick the HDMI input you connected the dock to with the source button on the remote. If there is no picture, see the troubleshooting answers below.
