Xiaomi TV Box S (2nd Gen) runs Google TV and outputs 4K over HDMI. First setup takes 10-15 minutes: the box asks for a language, a Wi-Fi network and a Google account.

## Connect the box to the TV and power

![A TV connected by an HDMI cable to a box that is plugged into a wall outlet](step-1.svg)

Plug one end of the HDMI cable into an HDMI port of the TV and the other end into the box. Connect the power adapter to the box and to a wall outlet.

## Put batteries into the remote

Take off the cover on the back of the remote, insert the batteries matching the plus and minus marks and close the cover.

## Select the HDMI input on the TV

Turn the TV on and use the source button to switch to the HDMI input the cable is in. The box welcome screen appears.

## Pair the remote if it does not respond

![A remote with two highlighted buttons: Back on the left and Home on the right](step-4.svg)

The remote is often paired at the factory. If it does not respond, hold **Back** and **Home** on it together until a confirmation appears on screen.

## Choose a language and region

Pick the interface language with the remote and confirm with the centre button. If you are asked for a region, choose yours.

## Sign in to your Google account

![A TV screen showing a square code and a smartphone pointed at it](step-6.svg)

A QR code appears on the screen. Turn on Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your phone, point the camera at the code and follow the prompts: the phone passes the Wi-Fi network and your Google account to the box.

With no phone at hand, press down on the remote and choose manual setup. Then you type the Wi-Fi password and the account details with the remote.

> [!NOTE]
> Google TV needs a Google account. If you do not have one, create it beforehand on a phone or computer.

## Wait for the home screen

Choose apps and subscriptions if the box offers it and wait for the Home screen. Now you can search for films and install apps.

## Check for system updates

Open {{Settings > System > About > System update}} and install updates if there are any. Do not unplug the box during installation.
