The PS5 DualSense connects to a computer two ways: wirelessly over Bluetooth, or with a USB Type-C cable. Sony supports Windows 10 (64-bit) and Windows 11.

If the computer has no Bluetooth, the cable is the easiest way. Jump to step 6.

## Turn on Bluetooth on the PC

![Windows settings window with the Bluetooth switch highlighted](step-1.svg)

Open {{Settings > Bluetooth & devices}} (on Windows 10 it is {{Settings > Devices > Bluetooth & other devices}}) and turn the Bluetooth switch on.

## Turn off the player indicator on the controller

If the player indicator on the controller is lit, hold the **PS** button until it goes out. Unplug the USB cable if one is connected.

## Put the controller in pairing mode

![Top view of a DualSense: the Create button left of the touchpad and the PS button at the bottom are highlighted, the light bar is blinking](step-3.svg)

Hold the **Create** button (left of the touchpad) and, still holding it, hold the **PS** button (below the sticks). Keep both pressed until the light bar around the touchpad starts blinking.

## Pick the controller in the Bluetooth list

![Windows device list with the Wireless controller row and the add button highlighted](step-4.svg)

In settings click **Add device**, choose **Bluetooth** and find "Wireless Controller" in the list. Click it.

> [!NOTE]
> Do not see the controller? Make sure the light bar is still blinking. If it has gone out, repeat step 3.

## Wait for the player indicator

When pairing finishes, the light bar blinks once more and then the player indicator lights up. The controller is ready.

## Or connect the controller with a cable

![A DualSense with a USB Type-C cable whose other end is plugged into a computer port](step-6.svg)

Plug the USB Type-C cable into the controller and the other end into a USB port on the PC. Windows recognises the controller by itself, no drivers are needed.

> [!TIP]
> Haptic feedback works on PC only over a cable and only in games that support it.

## Check the controller in a game

Start a game with controller support. In Steam, the "Controller Support" section on the game page shows whether the controller is fully or partially supported.
