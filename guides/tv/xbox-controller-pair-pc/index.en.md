Xbox controllers with Bluetooth connect to Windows 10 and 11 without wires or adapters. You pair once, and Windows remembers the controller after that.

## Check the controller's power

Put in fresh batteries or charge the battery pack. Low power is a common reason why the controller is not found.

## Turn Bluetooth on in the PC

![Windows Settings window with Bluetooth and devices open and the Bluetooth switch highlighted](step-2.svg)

Open {{Settings > Bluetooth & devices}} and turn the **Bluetooth** switch on.

## Switch the controller on with the Xbox button

![Xbox controller seen from the front, the central Xbox button highlighted](step-3.svg)

Press the Xbox button in the centre of the controller to turn it on.

## Hold the Pair button for three seconds

![Xbox controller seen from the top, the small Pair button on the top edge next to the port highlighted](step-4.svg)

Press the Pair button on the top edge of the controller between the bumpers and hold it for about three seconds: {{timer 3s}}. The Xbox button lights up and starts to blink: the controller is ready to pair.

## Click Add device

![Windows Settings window: the Add device row and its button on the right highlighted](step-5.svg)

In {{Settings > Bluetooth & devices}} click **Add device**.

## Choose the controller in the list

In the add device window choose **Bluetooth**, then find the Xbox controller in the list and click it. When Windows connects, the Xbox button stops blinking and stays lit.

## Test the controller

Press the Xbox button on the connected controller: the Controller bar with recent games opens. Start a game and check the controls.
