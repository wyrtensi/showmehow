Galaxy Buds pair over Bluetooth with any phone, but the full set of features needs the Galaxy Wearable app. A Samsung phone only needs the Connect pop-up; other Android phones need the app and a Samsung account.

New earbuds enter pairing mode on their own when you open the case. If they were paired with another device before, you have to start pairing mode by hand - see step 4 for each model.

## Turn on Bluetooth on the phone

![Phone screen with the Bluetooth switch turned on and a Bluetooth symbol](step-1.svg)

On a Samsung phone open {{Settings > Connections > Bluetooth}}, on other Android phones {{Settings > Connected devices}}. Turn Bluetooth on and keep the screen open.

## Install the Galaxy Wearable app

Required for a non-Samsung phone: install **Galaxy Wearable** from Google Play and have your Samsung account ready. On a Samsung phone you will need the app later for advanced earbud settings.

## Open the case with the earbuds inside

![Open Galaxy Buds case with two earbuds inside and an arrow showing the lid opening](step-3.svg)

Both earbuds must be in the case, and the case needs some charge. Open the lid and hold the case near the phone. New earbuds go straight into pairing mode.

## Start pairing mode if this is not the first pairing

![Underside of a Galaxy Buds case, a finger holding the button with a 3-second ring around it](step-4.svg)

The method depends on the model:

> [!VARIANT buds-first]
> Buds, Buds+, Buds Live, Buds Pro: put both earbuds in your ears and hold both touchpads for a few seconds. You hear a tone, then a steady pairing sound.

> [!VARIANT buds2]
> Buds2, Buds2 Pro: put both earbuds in the case, close and reopen the lid. Hold the touchpads of both earbuds at the same time for about 3 seconds {{timer 3s}}, until the case lights flash.

> [!VARIANT buds3]
> Buds3, Buds3 Pro: put the earbuds in the case, close and reopen the lid. Hold the Connect button on the underside of the case for at least 3 seconds {{timer 3s}}, until the lights flash green, blue and red.

> [!NOTE]
> Pairing mode lasts about 3 minutes. If you miss it, close the case and open it again.

## Confirm the connection on the phone

![Phone screen with a connection card at the bottom and the confirm button highlighted](step-5.svg)

### Samsung phone

In the pop-up tap Connect and follow the prompts.

### Another Android phone

Open the Galaxy Wearable app, tap Start and pick the earbuds on the scan screen. Then tap Pair and OK. If the app cannot find the earbuds, pick them in the Bluetooth list under {{Settings > Connected devices}} and confirm the connection.

## Allow access and wait for setup to finish

On a non-Samsung phone sign in to your Samsung account and allow the app's access requests. On a Samsung phone open the permissions section, read the guide and tap Next. Screen names can differ with the phone model and app version.

## Check the sound

Put the earbuds in and play some music. After the first pairing the earbuds connect to the phone by themselves every time you open the case.

> [!TIP]
> Advanced earbud settings, which depend on the model, are only in the Galaxy Wearable app (the earbud settings item). If you connected through the Bluetooth list, install the app later.
