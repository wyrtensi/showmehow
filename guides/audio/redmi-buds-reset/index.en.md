A reset erases the pairing with the phone and the link between the left and right earbud. After it the earbuds start again and wait for a connection. This guide is for Redmi Buds 4 Pro, whose button is on the bottom of the case.

## Remove the earbuds from the phone's Bluetooth list

![Earbuds page in Bluetooth settings with the unpair button highlighted](step-1.svg)

Open {{Settings > Bluetooth}}, tap **REDMI Buds 4 Pro** and choose "Unpair" (on a phone from another maker it may be called "Forget device").

## Put both earbuds in the case

![Open case with both earbuds inside and the indicator on](step-2.svg)

Both earbuds must be in the case. If one is lost the reset will not work: you need both.

## Find the button on the case

![Case upside down with the button next to the USB-C port highlighted](step-3.svg)

Turn the case over. On Redmi Buds 4 Pro the function button is on the bottom side, next to the USB-C port. On the regular Redmi Buds 4 it is on the back.

## Hold the button for more than 10 seconds

![Case front with the white indicator lit steadily](step-4.svg)

Keep holding until the white indicator on the front of the case **stays lit**. It takes more than 10 seconds.

## Release and wait

Let go of the button. The earbuds start by themselves, find each other and go into pairing mode.

## Pair the earbuds again

![Phone Bluetooth list with the earbuds entry highlighted](step-6.svg)

Open {{Settings > Bluetooth}}, find **REDMI Buds 4 Pro** and tap its name. If the phone asks for a passkey, enter `0000`.

> [!NOTE]
> If the two earbuds do not join after the reset they may have different firmware versions: update the firmware in the Xiaomi Earbuds app.
