faq:
  - q: The speaker is not in the list or will not connect
    a: If you paired it before, remove the speaker from the Bluetooth list on the phone and pair again. Disconnect any other device that is already connected to it. On Acton III, turn the speaker off, wait 10 seconds and turn it on again.
  - q: How many devices can be connected at once
    a: Emberton III and Acton III can hold two connected devices, but only one can play at a time - until the first is paused, the second will not start. Emberton III remembers up to 8 devices and reconnects to the last one when it is in range.
  - q: How do I factory reset Emberton III
    a: Remove EMBERTON III (and EMBERTON III [LE], if listed) from the phone's Bluetooth list. Turn the speaker on, hold the Bluetooth button for 7 seconds, until the indicator flashes red and the speaker restarts. Your settings are erased, firmware updates stay.
  - q: Where do I change sound settings and update the firmware
    a: In the Marshall Bluetooth app. You do not need it for pairing, but Marshall recommends it for settings and updates.

Marshall speakers pair like any Bluetooth speaker: start pairing mode, then pick the speaker on your phone. The buttons differ between models, so each step below has separate notes for Emberton III and Acton III.

> [!NOTE]
> On the original Emberton you switch on by holding the Control knob for 2 seconds, and pair with the Bluetooth button until a slow blue pulse; it shows up in the list as "Emberton".

## Switch the speaker on

![Marshall Emberton speaker with the power button at its top edge highlighted and a hold timer beside it](step-1.svg)

### Emberton III

Hold the power button until the speaker turns on.

### Acton III

Acton III runs on mains power: plug the power lead into the speaker and a wall socket, then turn the speaker on with its power switch.

## Turn on Bluetooth on the phone

Open {{Settings > Bluetooth}} on an iPhone or {{Settings > Connected devices}} on Android (on Samsung, {{Settings > Connections > Bluetooth}}) and turn Bluetooth on. Keep the screen open.

## Remove the old entry if you paired the speaker before

![Phone Bluetooth list, the info button on the speaker row highlighted and a remove button below](step-3.svg)

If the speaker was already paired with this phone, find it in the Bluetooth list, open its settings and forget the device. If it is not in the list, move on. Also disconnect any other device that is currently connected to the speaker.

## Put the speaker into pairing mode

![Marshall speaker with the Bluetooth button held and blue rings spreading from the indicator pulse](step-4.svg)

### Emberton III

Press and hold the Bluetooth button until the indicator shows a slow blue pulse.

### Acton III

Press the source button and choose Bluetooth - a lit indicator shows the active input. Then hold the source button for about 2 seconds, until the Bluetooth indicator starts to blink (Marshall describes it as a red pulse).

## Pick the speaker in the Bluetooth list

![Phone Bluetooth list with the speaker row highlighted and the connect button pressed](step-5.svg)

In the list of available devices tap **EMBERTON III** or **Acton III**. After a few seconds the phone shows that the speaker is connected.

## Check the sound

Play some music and set the volume. If two devices are connected to the speaker, only one can play at a time: pause the one that was playing first.
