The headphones remember up to 8 devices but can play from two at once. This guide covers first pairing and turning on the two-device mode.

## Turn on Bluetooth on the phone

![Phone screen with the Bluetooth switch turned on](step-1.svg)

Open {{Settings > Connections > Bluetooth}} and turn Bluetooth on. Keep the phone within a metre of the headphones.

## Put the headphones into pairing mode

![Left ear cup with the power button highlighted and the indicator blinking](step-2.svg)

If this is the first pairing, just turn the headphones on: they enter pairing mode by themselves. If other devices were paired before, hold the power button on the left ear cup for about **5 seconds**. The indicator flashes blue twice in a row and a voice says "Pairing".

## Pick WH-1000XM5 in the list

![Phone Bluetooth list with the WH-1000XM5 entry highlighted](step-3.svg)

Tap the headphones' name in the list of available devices. If the phone asks for a passkey, enter `0000`.

## Wait for the connection tone

When the link is made the headphones play a tone. If the list is empty, repeat step 2: pairing mode ends after 5 minutes.

## Install the Sony Headphones Connect app

Get **Sony | Headphones Connect** from Google Play or the App Store. It holds the headphone settings, including the two-device mode. Sony may rename the app in newer versions, so search by the manufacturer's name.

## Turn on the connection to two devices

![App screen with the two-device switch turned on](step-6.svg)

Connect the phone to the headphones and open the app. Turn on **Connect to 2 devices simultaneously**. The option sits in the headphone settings, under System or "My Device", depending on the app version.

## Connect the second device

![One pair of headphones connected to two phones at once](step-7.svg)

Pair the second device once, the same way as the phone (steps 1-3). Then connect it in Bluetooth. To switch sources, stop music on one device and start it on the other.

> [!NOTE]
> A call on either phone rings in the headphones. A third device cannot stay connected together with two others: the headphones drop the one that played music earlier.
