  - q: The speakers do not link with PartyBoost

There are two jobs here: first pair one speaker with your phone, then link it with a second one. The second part needs two speakers that support PartyBoost.

## Turn the speaker on

![JBL speaker from above with the power button highlighted](step-1.svg)

Press the power button on the top panel of the speaker. You hear the power-on tone.

## Press the Bluetooth button

![JBL speaker from above with the Bluetooth button highlighted](step-2.svg)

Press the button with the Bluetooth symbol next to the power button. The speaker enters pairing mode: its Bluetooth symbol flashes.

## Pick the speaker on the phone

![Phone Bluetooth list with the JBL speaker entry highlighted](step-3.svg)

Turn on Bluetooth in the phone settings and tap **JBL Flip 6** in the list. When it is done, "Connected" appears next to the name.

## Start some music

Play any track on the phone. The sound comes out of the speaker. For the second speaker to join, the music must already be playing.

## Press PartyBoost on the first speaker

![First JBL speaker with the PartyBoost button highlighted and a phone playing music beside it](step-5.svg)

Turn on the second speaker and put it close. On the first speaker press the **PartyBoost** button, the one with two chain links.

## Press PartyBoost on the second speaker

![Two JBL speakers, PartyBoost pressed on each, with a link between them](step-6.svg)

Press the PartyBoost button on the second speaker as well. After a few seconds both speakers play the same music. You can add more PartyBoost speakers the same way.

> [!TIP]
> The **JBL Portable** app holds the speaker settings and firmware updates. If linking fails, update both speakers first.
