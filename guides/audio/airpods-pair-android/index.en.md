AirPods pair with Android like ordinary Bluetooth earbuds: sound and the microphone work. Siri and automatic switching between Apple devices are not available.

## Turn on Bluetooth on the phone

![Phone screen with the Bluetooth switch turned on](step-1.svg)

Open {{Settings > Connections > Bluetooth}} (on some phones {{Settings > Connected devices}}) and turn Bluetooth on. Keep the screen open: the earbuds will appear here.

## Open the case with the earbuds inside

![Open case with two earbuds next to a phone](step-2.svg)

Both earbuds must be in the case. Open the lid and keep the case next to the phone.

## Put the case into pairing mode

![Back of the case with a round button and the front with a flashing light](step-3.svg)

The method depends on the model:

| Model | What to do |
|---|---|
| AirPods 1, 2, 3, Pro 1 and 2 | Press and hold the button on the back of the case for about 5 seconds |
| AirPods 4, 5, Pro 3 | Double-tap the front of the case |

Keep going until the light **flashes white**.

## Pick AirPods in the device list

![Phone Bluetooth list with the AirPods row highlighted](step-4.svg)

In the list of available devices tap the earbuds' name, for example "AirPods". After a few seconds the phone shows "Connected".

## Check the sound

Play some music and put the earbuds in. If sound still comes from the phone speaker, open the sound settings and choose AirPods as the output.

> [!NOTE]
> Apple does not show battery level or noise control modes on Android: you need a third-party app for that.
