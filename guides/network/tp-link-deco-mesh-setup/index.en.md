faq:
  - q: The Deco light does not blink blue
    a: Before you add a unit it must pulse blue. If it is yellow, wait 1-2 minutes while the unit starts up. If nothing changes, power cycle the unit. To put it back into setup mode, press and hold the reset pinhole for 1 second and wait until yellow turns into pulsing blue.
  - q: The app does not find the new unit
    a: Make sure your phone is connected to your Deco network, and that the new unit is next to the main one and pulses blue. If that does not help, see TP-Link's guide on errors when adding a Deco.
  - q: The light is red
    a: Red means a problem, for example the internet is lost. Restart the unit and check the internet connection. A pulsing red light on a satellite unit means it lost the link to the main Deco.
  - q: The signal is weak in a far room
    a: Move the extra unit closer to the main one. Too long a distance between units lowers performance.

A Deco mesh system is several identical units that form one Wi-Fi network all over the home. The whole setup runs in the Deco app on your phone.

## Install the Deco app

![A phone with the Deco app downloaded, an app icon and an install button on the screen](step-1.svg)

Find the Deco app in Google Play or the App Store and install it on your smartphone or tablet.

## Log in with your TP-Link ID

Open the app and log in with your TP-Link ID. If you do not have one, tap **Sign Up** and the app will guide you through it.

## Connect the first Deco to the modem

![A modem and a Deco unit joined by an Ethernet cable, with power-on arrows by both](step-3.svg)

Switch the modem off, connect the Deco to the modem with an Ethernet cable and switch both on. If you have no modem, plug the cable from the internet wall socket directly into the Deco.

## Wait for the pulsing blue light

![A Deco unit with its light: on the left steady yellow while starting, on the right pulsing blue](step-4.svg)

Right after power-on the light is yellow, which means the unit is starting up. In a minute or two it begins to pulse blue: the unit is ready for setup.

## Select the unit's location

In the app, choose the room where the unit stands. If it is not on the list, choose **Custom** and create your own.

## Set the internet connection type

Choose the connection type and enter the details. If you are not sure which to pick, ask your provider.

## Set the Wi-Fi name and password

![The Deco app form with two fields, network name and password, and a continue button below](step-7.svg)

Choose a network name and a password. You will use them to connect all your devices to Wi-Fi.

## Connect your phone to the new network

When the app asks, connect your phone to the Deco Wi-Fi to continue the setup. After that the network is running and you can connect your other devices to it.

## Add the second and further units

![The Deco app screen with the network tab, the plus icon highlighted in the corner](step-9.svg)

Switch on the extra unit next to the main one and wait for it to blink blue. In the app open the **Network** tab, tap **+** and choose **Add a Deco Unit**, then follow the prompts. On Deco X and XE models, units from the same box join by themselves within two minutes after power-on, once the main unit is set up.

## Check the lights of the units

Spread the units around the home, but not too far from the main one, or the link gets worse. A steady green or white light (depending on the model) means the unit works normally.
