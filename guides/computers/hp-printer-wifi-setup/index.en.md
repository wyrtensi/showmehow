Most HP printers connect to Wi-Fi through the HP Smart app: it passes the network name and password to the printer for you. The printer only needs to be in Wi-Fi setup mode. It is on for two hours during first-time setup, and you can turn it on again later.

This guide covers the most common models: DeskJet 2700-2900 and 4100-4300, ENVY 6000, Smart Tank. Other series have a different button layout.

## Put the printer next to the router and turn it on

It is best to keep the printer and the phone in the same room as the router. Turn the printer on, put in the cartridges and paper as the first-start screens ask.

## Install the HP app and sign in

![A phone showing an app store page with the Install button highlighted](step-2.svg)

Install the HP Smart app from your app store or from 123.hp.com, open it and sign in to your HP account or create one.

## Turn on Wi-Fi setup mode on the printer

![Control panel of an inkjet printer with the Cancel and Wireless buttons highlighted](step-3.svg)

For printers without a touchscreen, press **Cancel** to ready the printer, then hold **Wireless** and **Cancel** together until the Power button blinks.

If the printer has a touchscreen, touch the Setup or Wireless icon, choose **Network setup** or **Settings**, then **Restore Network Settings** and confirm the prompt.

## Wait a minute for the blue light to blink

![Printer panel with the Wireless light blinking and a one minute timer ring beside it](step-4.svg)

After 60 seconds the Wireless light starts blinking blue. This means the printer is ready to accept a Wi-Fi network. Now you have two hours.

## Add the printer in the app

![App screen with a found printer in the list and the add button highlighted](step-5.svg)

In HP Smart tap the plus, find your printer in the list and add it. The app asks which network to join.

## Choose the Wi-Fi network and enter the password

Select your network in the app, enter the password and wait for the confirmation. The printer connects by itself.

## Make sure the printer is connected

![A printer with a solid Wi-Fi light and a test page coming out](step-7.svg)

When the connection succeeds, the Wireless light stops blinking. Print a test page from the app to check the printer.

> [!NOTE]
> On a guest network, such as in a hotel, connecting the printer over the network may not work. Use Wi-Fi Direct.
