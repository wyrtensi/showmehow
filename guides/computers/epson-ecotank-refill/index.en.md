In Epson L-series printers the ink is poured straight into the tanks on the printer body. The procedure is the same for every colour. Be careful: ink easily stains clothes and tables.

> [!WARNING]
> Keep ink away from children. If the bottle was stored in the cold, let it warm up to room temperature for at least three hours before use.

## Prepare the bottle and protect the table

Check that the colour and the part code of the bottle match your printer. Put paper towels or newspaper under the work area. The printer must not be printing at this moment.

## Close the scanner cover

If the document cover is open, close it.

## Open the ink tank cover

![Front view of a printer with the cover of the ink tank unit lifted up](step-3.svg)

Open the cover of the ink tank unit on the printer. On some models first choose {{Maintenance > Fill Ink}} on the screen and read the precautions.

## Open the cap of the right tank

![Four ink tanks of different colours with one cap open and that tank highlighted](step-4.svg)

Open the cap of the tank you are filling. Make sure the tank colour matches the ink in the bottle.

## Take the cap off the bottle

Hold the bottle upright and slowly unscrew its cap. Do not spill the ink and do not touch the top of the bottle with your fingers.

## Insert the bottle and let the ink reach the upper line

![An ink bottle held upright over the filling port of a tank, the ink level rising to the upper dashed line](step-6.svg)

Find the upper line inside the tank. Insert the bottle straight down into the filling port. The ink flows by itself and stops at the upper line, you do not need to squeeze the bottle.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not fill above the upper line. If the ink does not flow, remove the bottle and insert it once more.

## Remove the bottle and close the tank cap

![An ink tank with the level between the lower and upper dashed lines and a closed cap](step-7.svg)

When the level reaches the upper line, take the bottle out. If ink is left in the bottle, tighten its cap and store it upright. Close the tank cap firmly.

## Close the cover and repeat for other colours

Close the cover of the tank unit firmly and repeat steps 4-7 for every colour you are refilling.

## Reset the ink level if the printer asks

If the printer asks which colours you refilled, select them. Models without a display need nothing.
