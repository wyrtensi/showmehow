Wireless Logitech mice usually connect in two ways: through a USB receiver (Logi Bolt or Unifying) and over Bluetooth. A receiver is handy on computers without Bluetooth, while Bluetooth does not use a USB port. The Easy-Switch button lets the mouse keep several connections and switch between them.

## Turn the mouse on

![Underside of a mouse with the power slider moved to on and highlighted](step-1.svg)

Turn the mouse over and move the slider on the underside to the on position. If the mouse does not turn on, replace the battery or charge it with the cable, if one came in the box.

## Plug the receiver into a USB port

![The side of a laptop and a small USB receiver being plugged into a port](step-2.svg)

To use the receiver, plug it into a free USB port on the computer. If the mouse was sold with a receiver, they are already linked and the cursor should move. Skip this step for Bluetooth.

## Put the mouse in pairing mode

![Underside of a mouse with the Easy-Switch button pressed and a channel light blinking](step-3.svg)

If the cursor does not move or you want Bluetooth, check the Easy-Switch lights on the mouse. If none is blinking, hold the Easy-Switch button for 3 seconds. The mouse is ready to pair when a light starts blinking.

## Open the Bluetooth settings on the computer

![Windows Bluetooth settings with the Add device button highlighted](step-4.svg)

Open {{Settings > Bluetooth & devices}} and turn Bluetooth on if it is off. Click **Add device**.

## Pick the mouse in the list

In the add device window choose **Bluetooth** and click the Logitech mouse in the list of available devices. Wait for the confirmation.

> [!NOTE]
> If the mouse is not found, check that the Easy-Switch light is still blinking. If it has gone out, repeat step 3.

## Pair the mouse with a new Logi Bolt receiver

You need this step only if you bought a separate Logi Bolt receiver. Install the Logi Options+ app and open it. Click **ADD DEVICE**, plug in the receiver and choose the Logi Bolt USB-C receiver option. Briefly press the connect button on the mouse, then hold it until the light blinks fast. Select the mouse in the app and press a few random buttons when it asks.

## Switch between computers

To pair the mouse with a second computer, press a different Easy-Switch button and repeat steps 3-5 on that computer. After that a short press of Easy-Switch switches the mouse between the computers.

## Install Logi Options+

Download the Logi Options+ app for your system if you want to customise the buttons and the speed. The mouse works without it, but Logitech recommends it instead of the older tools.
