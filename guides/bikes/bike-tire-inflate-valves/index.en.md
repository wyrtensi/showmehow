The right pressure makes riding easier and protects the tube and the rim. Tyres lose air even without a puncture - Schwalbe calls about 1 bar a month normal - so check the pressure at least once a month.

## Find the permitted pressure on the tyre sidewall

![Tyre sidewall with a printed pressure range between a minimum and a maximum](step-1.svg)

The sidewall has a permitted range printed on it. Never go below the minimum or above the maximum. Inside the range, choose by your weight with luggage and by feel: more load and a narrower tyre need more pressure.

## Identify the valve type

![Three valve types side by side: thin Presta, wider Schrader and Dunlop](step-2.svg)

Bicycles use three types:

- **Presta** (Schwalbe calls it Sclaverand, SV, also French) - thin, 6 mm, with a knurled nut at the tip.
- **Schrader** (AV, car valve) - like a car tyre valve, 8 mm.
- **Dunlop** (DV, also Woods) - the most common in the world, with a nut and an inner core.

## Unscrew the nut on a Presta valve

![Thin Presta valve with its knurled nut being unscrewed by hand](step-3.svg)

On a Presta valve first loosen the knurled nut at the tip - beginners often struggle with it. A Schrader valve does not need this. On a Dunlop valve the nut and the core must be in place before you inflate.

> [!WARNING]
> The thin pin at the tip of a Presta valve bends easily when you attach or remove the pump head. Put the head on straight.

## Push the pump head on and close the lever

![Pump head pushed onto the valve, the clamp lever raised](step-4.svg)

Push the head straight onto the valve as far as it goes and flip the clamp lever. If the pump has two openings, use the one that fits your valve.

## Pump to the right pressure on the gauge

![Floor pump with a pressure gauge, the needle reaching the target mark](step-5.svg)

Pump while watching the gauge. Stop inside the range from the sidewall. Schwalbe recommends a floor pump with a built-in gauge to anyone who rides regularly.

## Remove the head and close the valve

Release the lever and take the head off straight, without tilting it. Screw the nut down on a Presta valve and put the cap on. Tighten the rim nut by hand only: pliers can damage the tube.

## Check how the tyre sits on the rim

Look all the way round the tyre: the guide line near the rim should run parallel to it. If the tyre bulges on one side, let the air out, centre it by hand and inflate again.

Tyres lose air over time, so check the pressure at least once a month, as Schwalbe advises.
