A Xiaomi scooter has to be assembled, paired with the app and activated before the first ride. The steps follow the manual for the 4 Pro Max; the pairing order is the same in the Xiaomi help article for the 5 Pro. On other models the pictures may differ, and some come with the handlebar already fitted - then skip steps 3 and 4.

> [!NOTE]
> The app is called Mi Home, and in Europe except Russia it is Xiaomi Home. Go by the name your phone shows.

## Stand the scooter up and put the kickstand down

![Electric scooter standing on the ground with its kickstand folded down](step-1.svg)

Put the scooter on level ground and put the kickstand down so that it stands firmly.

## Raise the stem and lock the lever

![Folding joint at the base of the stem: the lever pulled up and the stem raised](step-2.svg)

Lift the stem up, then pull the quick release lever up to lock it in place.

## Fit the handlebar on the stem

Put the handlebar on the stem with the headlight facing forward.

## Tighten the four handlebar screws

![Handlebar clamp on the stem with four screws and a T-shaped hex key](step-4.svg)

Tighten the four screws on the stem with the T-shaped key from the box, in the order of the numbers in the manual's picture. The manual gives a torque of 3.0 plus or minus 0.3 N·m.

## Charge the scooter

Connect the charger. The light on the charger changes from red to green when the scooter is fully charged. It is best to charge it before the first ride.

## Install the app

Scan the QR code on the label on the control panel (it is also on the right side of the deck and on the back cover of the manual) and install the Mi Home or Xiaomi Home app. Sign in to your account.

## Switch the scooter on

![Handlebar panel: the round power button is pressed and the scooter gives a signal](step-7.svg)

Press the power button on the control panel. Until the scooter is activated it will keep beeping and its speed will be limited to 10 km/h. To switch it off, hold the button for about three seconds.

## Add the scooter in the app

![Phone with a plus button scanning a square QR code on the scooter](step-8.svg)

Open the app, tap the plus in the top right corner and choose {{Add a device}} or {{Scan}}. Scan the QR code on the control panel and follow the prompts to add and pair the scooter.

## Watch the video and tap Activate

The app offers a video for beginners. Watch it to the end - if you leave early, activation fails. Then tap the activate button. After that the beeping stops and the speed limit is lifted.

## Set off safely

![One foot on the deck while the other pushes off the ground](step-10.svg)

Wear a helmet, elbow pads and knee pads. Check the tyre pressure: Xiaomi recommends 40-50 psi. Step on the deck with one foot and push off gently with the other. The accelerator starts working once your speed is above 5 km/h. To brake, release the accelerator and squeeze the brake lever. When you park, put the kickstand down.
