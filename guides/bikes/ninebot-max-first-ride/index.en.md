This guide follows the manufacturer's manual for the Ninebot KickScooter Max G30. The scooter arrives almost assembled: you unfold the stem, fit the handlebar, charge it, connect the app and activate it. Without activation the scooter goes only at a very low speed.

Charging takes about 6.5 hours, so put the scooter on charge in advance.

> [!WARNING]
> Always ride in a helmet. Make your first ride where there are no children, pedestrians, animals, cars or bicycles. Do not ride in the rain or on wet roads.

## Unfold the stem and lock it

![The scooter stem is unfolded and locked by the lever, the kickstand is out](step-1.svg)

Switch the scooter off. Unhook the snap hook from the buckle, raise the handlebar stem, close the quick release lever and turn the safety hook clockwise. Then unfold the kickstand so that the scooter stands.

## Connect the wires and fit the handlebar

![The handlebar goes onto the stem with the headlight facing forward, the wires connected inside](step-2.svg)

Firmly connect the wires inside the handlebar and the stem. Then slide the handlebar onto the stem, paying attention to the right orientation: the headlight must face forward.

## Fix the handlebar with six screws

![Six screws around the handlebar clamp and a hex wrench](step-3.svg)

Tighten the six M5 screws from the kit with the hex wrench. Afterwards check that the scooter can be switched on and off.

## Charge the scooter

Open the charge port cover, plug in the charger and close the cover when finished. Use only the charger supplied by Segway and do not charge if the port or the cable is wet. A full charge takes about 6.5 hours. The scooter is charged when the LED on the charger changes from red to green.

## Switch the scooter on

![The scooter dashboard: the power button is highlighted, with battery bars on the display](step-5.svg)

Press the power button on the dashboard. If the scooter is not activated it will beep from time to time. To switch it off, hold the button for 3 seconds. Five bars on the display show the battery level, each about 20 percent.

## Install the app

On a phone with Bluetooth 4.1 or newer, install the **Segway-Ninebot** app, register and sign in.

## Connect the scooter over Bluetooth

![The phone searches for the scooter over Bluetooth, the scooter is next to it](step-7.svg)

Switch the scooter on: a blinking Bluetooth icon means it is waiting for a connection. In the app choose {{Vehicle > Search vehicle}}. When the connection succeeds the scooter beeps, and the Bluetooth icon stops blinking and stays lit.

## Activate the scooter

Follow the prompts of the app to activate the scooter and learn how to ride safely. You must read Riding Safety in the app before the first ride.

## Get ready for the ride

Put on a helmet and protective gear. The scooter is designed for riders aged 14 and over, weighing 30 to 100 kg and 120 to 200 cm tall. Before every ride check for loose fasteners and damaged parts. Pick a flat place away from people and cars. The tyres should be inflated to 32-37 psi.

## Push off and ride

![A rider in a helmet stands with one foot on the deck and pushes off with the other, both hands on the handlebar](step-10.svg)

Stand on the footrest with one foot and push off with the other to start gliding. When you are balanced, put the other foot on the footrest and press the throttle with your right hand. The motor engages only after about 5 km/h (3.1 mph). Slow down by releasing the throttle and squeezing the brake lever. To turn, shift your body and turn the handlebar slightly. For the first rides pick ECO mode: a double press of the power button switches modes.
