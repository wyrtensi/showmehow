Special salt softens the water inside the dishwasher and protects it from scale, and rinse aid helps dishes dry without streaks. Fill both at the first start and then as they run low.

This guide follows the Bosch SMS46NI20Q quick reference guide. Other models may have different dispensers and buttons.

## Find the salt compartment at the bottom of the tub

![Front view of the dishwasher tub, the round lid of the salt compartment near the bottom is highlighted](step-1.svg)

Open the door and find the round lid near the bottom of the tub. If the lower rack is in the way, pull it out.

## Unscrew the compartment lid

Unscrew the lid of the salt compartment and take it off.

## Fill the compartment with water at the first start

At the very first start, fill the compartment to the top with water first. Later there is no need to add water: you can top up the salt even if there is water inside.

## Pour in special salt through the funnel

![Funnel in the opening of the salt compartment, salt pouring in from a bag](step-4.svg)

Put the funnel in and add salt until the compartment is full. The water inside is displaced and runs out, this is normal. Use only special dishwasher salt. Never put detergent into this compartment.

## Close the lid and start a programme right away

Put the lid back and screw it shut. The salt must be added right before a programme starts, otherwise it can damage the machine. So load the dishes and start a wash straight away.

## Open the rinse aid dispenser

![Rinse aid dispenser on the door, an arrow presses the catch and lifts the lid](step-6.svg)

Press the catch on the lid of the rinse aid dispenser and lift the lid.

## Pour rinse aid up to the max mark

![Open rinse aid dispenser filled up to the max line while a bottle pours](step-7.svg)

Slowly pour in liquid rinse aid up to the **max** mark. Do not overfill: the excess can leak when the lid closes. Wipe up anything you spill.

## Close the dispenser lid

Press the lid down until it clicks. The click means it is closed tightly.

## Find out your water hardness

You can ask your water supplier or measure it with test strips. The machine takes the values H00-H07. Use the table from the quick guide (units are millimoles per litre):

| Hardness | mmol/l | Setting on the machine |
|---|---|---|
| Soft | 0 - 1.1 | H00 |
| Soft | 1.2 - 1.4 | H01 |
| Medium | 1.5 - 1.8 | H02 |
| Medium | 1.9 - 2.1 | H03 |
| Medium | 2.2 - 2.9 | H04 |
| Hard | 3.0 - 3.7 | H05 |
| Hard | 3.8 - 5.4 | H06 |
| Hard | 5.5 - 8.9 | H07 |

The manual also has a table of country-specific units, such as degrees of hardness.

## Set the hardness on the panel

![Dishwasher panel with digits on the display and two highlighted buttons on the right](step-10.svg)

1. Press the power button.
2. Hold programme button A and press Start repeatedly until the display shows H:xx.
3. Release button A and Start. Programme button A flashes.
4. Press programme button C repeatedly until the right value is shown.
5. Press Start to save the setting.
