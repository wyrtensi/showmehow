faq:
  - q: White smoke comes out of the air fryer
    a: It is usually grease that heated up on the bottom of the pan, or breading stuck to the heating element. Stop cooking, soak up the excess fat with a paper towel and carry on. After every use, wash the pan, the basket and the heating element.
  - q: It smokes a little the very first time
    a: For a new air fryer this is allowed, the Philips manual says some smoke may appear on first use. If the smoke does not stop, switch the appliance off and wash the pan.
  - q: The display shows E1
    a: The appliance may have been stored somewhere cold. Let it stand at room temperature for at least 15 minutes and switch it on again. If E1 stays, contact Philips service.
  - q: The food is not crispy
    a: Do not overload the basket and spread the food in a single layer, shake the basket halfway, pat wet food dry and add only a little oil, directly on the food.
  - q: Can I line the basket with baking paper or foil
    a: Philips does not recommend it. Paper and foil block the airflow, and without food on top they can be pulled into the heating element.

An air fryer cooks with hot air, so it needs little oil or none at all. This guide covers the digital Philips Airfryer 3000 Series models (for example HD9252) with temperature, time and On/Off buttons. Older models have two knobs instead: one for temperature and one for the timer.

You do not need to preheat it, the food can go in right away.

## Remove the packaging and stickers

Take the air fryer out of the box, remove all packing material inside and out, and peel off any stickers on the housing. Leave the rubber plug in the pan, it keeps the basket in place.

## Wash the pan and the basket

Before first use, thoroughly clean the parts that touch food: the pan and the basket. Use warm water, dishwashing liquid and a soft sponge, or a dishwasher. Do not use metal scourers or abrasives, they damage the non-stick coating. Wipe the outside with a damp cloth and dry the control panel.

## Place the air fryer on a level, stable surface

![Air fryer seen from the front on a counter, free space around it and nothing on top](step-3.svg)

You need a horizontal, level and heat-resistant surface. Put nothing on top or on the sides: it blocks the airflow. Do not run the appliance right against a wall or under a cupboard, steam can damage them. Keep it away from a gas or electric stove.

## Put the food in the basket

![Pulled-out pan with the basket, food in the basket just below the MAX line](step-4.svg)

Pull the pan with the basket out by the handle and put the food in the basket. Do not go above the **MAX** mark or above the amount in the food table of the manual. For frozen fries, about two thirds of the basket is a good guide. Never pour oil into the pan. If you want a crisp crust, lightly oil the food itself.

## Slide the pan with the basket back in

Push the pan in all the way. Never use the pan without the basket in it. Then plug the cord into the wall outlet.

## Switch on and choose the temperature

![Air fryer control panel with the On/Off button and the temperature buttons highlighted](step-6.svg)

Press <kbd>On/Off</kbd>, then use the temperature up and down buttons to pick the value. Starting points from the manual (these are suggestions, ingredients differ):

| Food | Amount | Temperature | Time, min |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thin frozen fries | 200-500 g | 180 °C | 16-22 |
| Home-made fries | 200-500 g | 180 °C | 18-26 |
| Frozen chicken nuggets | 200-600 g | 200 °C | 10-15 |
| Chicken drumsticks | 2-6 pieces | 180 °C | 18-24 |
| Fish fillet | 1-3 pieces | 160 °C | 22-25 |

> [!WARNING]
> Do not fry fresh potatoes above 180 °C, it produces more acrylamide.

## Set the time and start cooking

![Control panel with the time up button and the On/Off button highlighted](step-7.svg)

Press the time up button until the display shows the minutes you need, then press <kbd>On/Off</kbd> again to start. While cooking, the display alternates between temperature and time, and the last minute counts down in seconds. You can change the temperature and time at any moment with the up and down buttons. If you do not set a time within 30 minutes, the appliance switches itself off.

## Shake the food halfway through

![Pan with the basket pulled out by the handle, shaking arrow above the basket](step-8.svg)

Fries, nuggets, wings and other food in several layers cook more evenly when mixed. Pull the pan with the basket out by the handle (the appliance pauses by itself), put it on a heat-resistant stand, slide the cover, press the basket release button and shake the basket over the sink. Put the basket back into the pan and slide it into the appliance: cooking carries on.

> [!CAUTION]
> The pan, the basket and the inside get very hot. Handle them only by the handle, preferably with oven gloves.

## Take the cooked food out

When the timer rings, pull out the pan and check whether the food is ready. If not, slide it back in and add a few minutes. Lift the basket out of the pan and tip the food into a bowl. There may be hot oil in the pan, so do not turn the pan with its contents over. Use tongs for small or fragile food. Then switch the appliance off with <kbd>On/Off</kbd>, unplug it and let it cool down before cleaning.
