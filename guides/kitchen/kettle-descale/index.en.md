Scale builds up on the bottom and the heating element, so the kettle boils slower and may switch off early. This guide follows the Bosch manual: boiled water plus a little white vinegar.

The work itself takes about ten minutes, the rest of the time the vinegar does its job: leave it in the kettle for several hours.

## Fill the kettle with water up to 1.7

![Kettle with a level scale, water filled up to the top 1.7 mark](step-1.svg)

Pour fresh water up to the **1.7** mark on the scale. Do not go above the maximum, the water may boil over. Fill it with water only: milk and instant drinks damage the kettle.

## Boil the water

![Kettle on its base, the switch is lit and steam rises from the spout](step-2.svg)

Close the lid until it clicks, put the kettle on its base and push the switch down. It lights up. The kettle switches itself off once the water has boiled. It must only run with the lid closed and the limescale filter in place.

## Add a little white vinegar

![A bottle pouring white vinegar into the kettle with hot water](step-3.svg)

Lift the kettle off the base and wait a few minutes: the water is very hot. Open the lid with the release button and add a small amount of white vinegar. The Bosch manual does not give an exact amount.

> [!WARNING]
> Open the lid carefully and do not lean over the steam. Hold the kettle by the handle only.

## Leave the solution for several hours

![A clock next to the kettle, the solution stands without heating](step-4.svg)

Close the lid and do not switch the kettle on. Leave the solution for several hours so the vinegar can dissolve the scale. The evening is a good time: leave it overnight.

## Pour it out and rinse the kettle

Pour out the water with vinegar and rinse the kettle and the limescale filter with clear water a few times. Do not scrape off any scale that remains with hard objects: they damage the seal. Wipe the outside of the kettle and the base with a damp cloth only and never put them in water.

> [!TIP]
> If you still smell vinegar, fill the kettle with clean water, boil it and pour it away. This tip comes from experience, not from the manual.

## Clean the removable limescale filter

![The removable limescale filter lowered into a cup with a little vinegar](step-6.svg)

Take out the removable filter and soak it in a little vinegar, then rinse it with clear water and put it back. The kettle must not be used without the filter: it may fail to switch off on its own.
