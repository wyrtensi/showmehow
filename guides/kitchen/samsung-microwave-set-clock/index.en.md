Samsung microwaves set the clock in different ways: some have a dedicated Clock button, others hide the time setting under another button. First follow the most common path with the Clock button, other layouts are collected in the table at the end.

## Find the Clock button on the panel

![Microwave from the front, door on the left and a panel with a display and buttons on the right, the panel is highlighted](step-1.svg)

Look at the control panel to the right of the door. You need a button labelled **Clock**. If there is none, go to the table in the last step.

## Press Clock

![Enlarged microwave panel, the Clock button is highlighted next to the display](step-2.svg)

Press <kbd>Clock</kbd> briefly. If nothing happens, press and hold the button for 3 seconds. On some models one press is enough.

## Key in the time on the number pad

![Microwave number pad, the digits to press are highlighted](step-3.svg)

Enter the hours and minutes as digits one after another, for example 7, 3, 0 for 7:30.

## Pick AM or PM if your model asks for it

On models with AM/PM, press <kbd>Clock</kbd> again to choose the right one. If your model has no such step, skip it.

## Confirm with Enter/Start or OK

![Microwave panel with the Enter/Start confirm button highlighted at the bottom](step-5.svg)

Press <kbd>Enter/Start</kbd>. If there is no such button, press <kbd>OK</kbd>. The display shows the time you set.

## Use another layout if there is no Clock button

Samsung describes four more ways. Find your button on the panel:

| Button on the panel | What to press |
|---|---|
| Options (ME21M706BA) | Press Options, then 9. Press OK, enter the time, press OK |
| Arrows and OK (MG11T5018CC) | Press OK. Set the hour with the left or right arrow, press OK. Set the minutes the same way, press OK |
| Power Level (ME19R7041F) | Hold Power Level for 3 seconds, enter the time. Press Power Level once for AM or twice for PM. Confirm with OK/Start |
| My Settings (MC17J8000CS) | Press My Settings, then 9. Press OK, enter the time, press OK |

> [!NOTE]
> Button names and the sequence depend on the model. If none of these fit, find the manual by model number on the Samsung site.
