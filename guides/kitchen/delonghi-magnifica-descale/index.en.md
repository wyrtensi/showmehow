When the descale light on the panel blinks red, it is time to descale the machine. This guide follows the De'Longhi manual for the Magnifica S ECAM 22.110, where the solution runs through the cappuccino maker and the coffee spouts. Other models may label the buttons differently, so check your own manual.

Plan for about an hour: the programme itself runs for 25-30 minutes, plus preparation and two rinses.

> [!WARNING]
> Hot water with acid comes out of the cappuccino maker and the spouts. Keep your hands away and do not lean over the machine while the programme runs.

## Make sure it is time to descale

![Coffee machine control panel with the red descale light blinking](step-1.svg)

The machine tells you itself: the descale light {{led red blink}} on the panel blinks red. Read the label on the descaler pack. Use only De'Longhi descaler: another product can damage the machine and the guarantee will not cover it.

## Switch the machine on and empty the trays

Switch the coffee machine on and wait until it is ready. Empty the drip tray and the grounds container and put them back.

## Empty the tank and take out the filter

Pull out the water tank and pour all the water away. If a water softener filter is fitted, take it out: you must not descale with the filter in place.

## Add the descaler and the water

![Water tank with two marks: descaler up to the lower mark, then water up to the upper one](step-4.svg)

Pour the descaler into the tank up to level **A**, which matches a 100 ml pack and is marked on the inner surface of the tank. Then add water up to level **B** and put the tank back in the machine.

## Put a container under the spouts

![A large container placed under the cappuccino maker and the coffee spouts](step-5.svg)

Place a container of at least 2 litres under the cappuccino maker and the coffee spouts. The solution will run out into it.

## Start the programme with the button

![The descale button on the panel is highlighted, with a stopwatch next to it](step-6.svg)

Wait until the coffee lights are on steadily. Then press and hold the descale button for at least **5 seconds** {{timer 5s}} to confirm that you added the solution. The button light stays on and the programme starts.

## Turn the dial to I

![The hot water and steam dial turned to position I, with a stopwatch next to it](step-7.svg)

When the light for the hot water and steam dial flashes, turn the dial to **I**. The solution comes out of the cappuccino maker and the coffee spouts, and the machine makes pauses and rinses by itself. It takes about 25-30 minutes and needs nothing from you.

{{timer 25m}}

When the programme ends, the lights on the panel start to flash.

## Stop the flow and rinse the tank

Turn the dial back to **0**. Empty the container and the drip tray. Take out the tank, empty it and rinse it under running water, fill it with fresh water up to **MAX** and put it back. Then press and hold the button for at least 5 seconds {{timer 5s}} until its light flashes: the machine is ready to rinse.

## Do the first rinse

![Clean water runs from the spouts into the container and the steam dial is highlighted](step-9.svg)

Put an empty container under the cappuccino maker and the spouts and turn the dial to **I**. Hot water runs first from the cappuccino maker and then from the spouts. When the tank is empty the lights flash: turn the dial back to **0**.

## Do the second rinse

Empty the container. Take out the tank, put the filter back in if you removed one, fill the tank with fresh cold water up to **MAX** and put it back. Place an empty container under the cappuccino maker only and turn the dial to **I**. When the rinse finishes, a light on the panel signals it: turn the dial back to **0**.

## Refill the tank and carry on

Fill the tank with fresh water up to **MAX** and put it back. Descaling is done and the descale light goes out.
