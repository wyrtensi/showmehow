The filters on the floor of a Bosch dishwasher catch food scraps so they do not end up on your dishes or in the pump. When they clog, water circulates worse and the dishes come out dirty. They are easy to clean: the system consists of a coarse filter, a fine filter and a micro filter that come apart by hand.

Follow your own model: the construction is similar across machines, but the locks and arrows may look different.

## Take out the lower rack

Open the door and pull the lower rack all the way out. Put it aside so that it does not get in the way. If you have just run a cycle, let the machine cool down first.

## Find the filter on the tub floor

![Top view of the dishwasher tub: the filter system sits in a recess in the floor](step-2.svg)

The filter sits in a recess in the tub floor. It is a round part with a handle, with arrow marks on it and next to it.

## Turn the coarse filter and lift the system out

![The coarse filter is turned counterclockwise and lifted out together with the rest of the system](step-3.svg)

Turn the coarse filter **counterclockwise** (to the left) and lift the whole system out. Make sure no debris falls into the recess underneath.

## Remove the micro filter

![The flat micro filter is pulled down and off the lower part of the system](step-4.svg)

Pull the micro filter downward and off the filter.

## Separate the coarse and the fine filter

![Two locking catches on the coarse filter are pressed to release it from the fine filter](step-5.svg)

Press the locking catches on the coarse filter and lift it up and out of the fine filter. Carefully remove any debris from between the coarse and fine filters.

## Rinse the parts

![A filter under a running tap with a soft brush next to it](step-6.svg)

Rinse all parts under running water. If they are very dirty, scrub them with a soft brush in warm soapy water. Clean each part on its own. Then wipe them or let them dry.

> [!TIP]
> While the filter is out, look into the recess on the floor: if bones, glass or labels have gathered there, remove them.

## Put the system together

Assemble it in reverse: first the coarse filter into the fine one until the locking catches on the coarse filter click, then fit the micro filter back on.

## Install the filter and turn it clockwise

![The filter system turned clockwise so that the arrow marks on the filter and on the floor line up](step-8.svg)

Insert the assembled system into the recess and turn the coarse filter **clockwise** (to the right). Stop when the arrow marks line up: the system is locked in.

## Put the rack back

Put the lower rack back in and close the door. The filters are ready.
